## Usable travel: current S3 envelope illustration only

The primary's current S3 candidate has aft hardware at nose-to-aft station
**1227 mm**. Retain the existing forward lug front at**250 mm**, second lug
front430 mm, and12 mm lug lengths. The lighter revision has **no adopted new
geometry**, so do not move those positions or call the cylindrical lugs rail
buttons. Exact1010/1515 buttons, attachments, spacing and mass remain unspecified.

With the explicitly **assumed100 mm** aft-hardware height above the bottom of a
clear guide, the upper/front lug leading edge starts at:

`100 +(1227 −250) =1077 mm above guide bottom`.

The conservative two-guide restraint boundary is first departure of that upper
leading edge. It loses **1.077 m** of an overall guide before useful travel is
counted. The later single-guide interval earns no safety credit. Real last
engagement can differ with the12 mm lug length or actual button head/slot/end
geometry; stops, end caps, occupied slot, deflector, stand-off and loading
orientation can remove additional travel. The100 mm offset has not been measured
at either club.

For **illustration only**, an unobstructed10 ft overall guide is3.048 m long,
so `3.048 −1.077 =1.971 m` conservative travel under those assumptions. This is
**not confirmed equipment**, a departure-speed result or an adopted guide. Source73's
earlier1212 mm tail gave a different loss; its conversion must not be reused as
though it described S3. Equally, one cannot credit a10 ft rail with3 m usable.

Keeping this placement/offset,1.5 m usable requires at least2.577 m clear overall;
1.8 m requires2.877 m. The baseline remains**1.5 m usable**. A1.8 m usable fallback
requires measured clear geometry, a compatible specified attachment and confirmed
supported pad access. More guide length does not close guide/lug strength,
receiver, thermal, stability or recovery acceptance.

## Wind, motor state and range acceptance

MDARS says it operates under Tripoli standards; ROC says it follows NAR and
Tripoli codes. Tripoli's current Unified Safety Code, version2 effective
January2026, forbids launch when sustained surface wind exceeds20 mph and permits
the RSO to reject a flight. A stable guiding platform, sufficient thrust and
inspection are required. This code ceiling is not this rocket's qualified wind
limit; gusts, deflection, recovery drift and RSO judgment can require less.
No lower standing MDARS numerical limit was located. ROC's dated gust rejection
above is an operational example, not evidence that20 mph is suitable for S3.
[Official Tripoli code,7-1/7-3/8-1/9-3](https://www.tripoli.org/content.aspx?club_id=795696&module_id=520420&page_id=22).

ROC specifically requires RSO/FSR approval for each flight, stable flight/safe
recovery and OSFM motor evidence; its page accepts a state stamp on the motor,
packaging or paperwork. High-power motors carry separate California operator and
user-certification requirements. It instructs F-and-above igniters to be installed
at the pad subject to listed exceptions, and recovery electronics to be safed,
shunted or off at inspection. [ROC range rules](https://rocstock.org/flying-with-roc-2/).

No exact motor, lot, delay, hardware or California**model-motor** classification
has been accepted for this project. G80 remains the first engineering hypothesis;
G54 remains conditional. A general G designation or national numerical eligibility
screen does not establish California/club classification. Provide the exact
motor's current certification, source/variant mapping, assembled envelope/mass,
California seal/classification and recovery/delay plan to the club. The existing
G80 casing-versus-assembled-envelope mapping remains unresolved, not a proven
124-vs128 mm contradiction. No new motor approval claim is made here.

MDARS's certification-preparation page requests CG, CP, simulated altitude and
delay/electronic deployment information. This is useful evidence of the kind of
flight package its range expects, not acceptance of a G flight or a requirement
to obtain HPR certification for a qualifying model motor. Confirm the exact
project's participation and review process. [MDARS preparation guidance](https://www.mojavedesertrocketry.org/l1-l2-l3-cert).
