## One process lead and its evidence boundary

WEST's [105/206 sheet](https://www.westsystem.com/app/uploads/2022/12/105-206-Epoxy-Resin.pdf) supports general wood/composite wet-out and bonding. It gives **5:1 resin:hardener by volume** (5.36:1 by weight), **16°C minimum recommended application temperature**, **90–110 minutes thin-film working time**, **10–15 hours to solid thin-film cure** and **1–4 days to working strength** under its stated conditions. The [surface-preparation guidance](https://www.westsystem.com/instruction/epoxy-basics/surface-preparation/) calls for clean, dry surfaces and abrasion of smooth cured/nonporous faces; contamination is removed before sanding. It specifies 80-grit for hardwood and water removal of cured-epoxy amine blush before sanding. Do not abrade the thin LOC paper wall by a generic wood rule without product guidance and a controlled witness.

The **proposed RP1 process** is to identify and condition exact stock, prepare both adherends under manufacturer guidance, wet out porous wood/paper with correctly metered neat 105/206, then apply 105/206 thickened with 406-2 to a non-sag fillet. Meter mix, bondline, coverage, clamping, temperature, humidity, batch/lot and before/after mass on witnesses. The current WEST [bonding/clamping URL](https://www.westsystem.com/instruction/epoxy-basics/bonding-and-clamping/) timed out in this bounded check; earlier [local material notes](wo03-rp1-joint-material-reconciliation.md) record its starvation warning. The exact filler loading, paper-tube preparation, fillet profile and clamp pressure therefore remain supplier questions, not issued instructions.

For a later owner-reviewed article protocol, **7 days at 22 ± 2°C before evaluation** is a proposed conservative conditioning rule, **not** the WEST TDS's 1–4 day working-strength statement and **not** evidence of loaded-joint acceptance. The owner-reviewed plan must set moisture, temperature, replication, fixtures, calibration and separate held-out specimens. Keep the existing **45°C substrate screen**. The TDS's resin heat-deflection/Tg values do not establish a loaded wood/paper/tube joint allowable at that temperature. Follow current SDS and site safety procedures before any later authorized mixing, dust generation or load test; no work is directed by this document.
