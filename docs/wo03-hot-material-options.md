# WO-03 hot-material options: bounded evidence for the next trade

**Purpose.** This is a shortlist for analysis and coupon screening, not a material selection or design allowable. The current upper-v2 thermal model estimates a 225 °C liner peak, 88 °C mount, 72 °C inner gimbal, 64 °C outer gimbal and 59–64 °C body. These are model outputs, not measured temperatures; the present thermal model does not qualify transient interfaces, local hot spots, bond lines, or repeated service. In particular, a catalog maximum-use temperature or adhesive Tg does not establish strength under this part's load, duration, cycling, geometry, or cure.

## Evidence boundaries and next checks

- The liner maximum from the current calculation (225 °C) is above the G-7 published maximum (220 °C), while below the silicone-bonded mica supplier rating (500 °C continuous). This comparison only narrows candidates; it does not predict actual liner life or validate the thermal model.
- The model's 88 °C mount, 72 °C inner gimbal and 64 °C outer gimbal are below G-11 Class F's stated 150 °C application class, but temperature alone cannot close structural suitability. G-11 supplier values do not capture this assembly's laminate direction, stress concentrations, fastener bearing, shock, vibration, bond degradation, or hot dwell.
- A Tg (or HDT) marks a property transition/test result, not a loaded continuous-use rating. The Wood-Bond page's 163 °C operating claim does not supersede its 93 °C Tg or metal-only lap-shear test. EL-315's quoted Tg depends on hardener and cure.
- For each surviving candidate, the next evidence should be exact grade/thickness/lot documentation; process and cure instructions; representative hot coupon tests with the actual adherends, surface prep, bondline and fasteners; and instrumented thermal correlation. Do not infer packed mass, exact fit, allowable strength, or service life from these catalog sheets.
- Axial/buckling strength data for the actual kraft airframe and a directly applicable model-rocket shell were not identified in this small manufacturer-focused source set. No shell allowable is inferred here.
