## Separate software-validation stops

These failures are not uncertain material, thermal-boundary or flight inputs.
Better physical data cannot repair them. The final native CCX bridge case passed
its input mesh/area checks but missed support equilibrium by **104.697 N** and
**22.093 N** in its two steps; its displacement, stress and work outputs remain
unvalidated, and the cause is unidentified. The Elmer classic and vector paths
agreed with each other within about `5.4e-12 K` on fixture A, while both failed
the unchanged `1e-7 K` exact-field criterion at all three levels; actual-baffle
accuracy and energy balances remain unavailable. These are prerequisites to
relying on subsequent structural or thermal numerical fields, not evidence that
physical inputs caused the failures. See the [CCX final diagnostic](wo03-g80-final-coarse-diagnostic.md)
and [Elmer classic comparison](wo03-elmer-classic-comparison.md).

G54 and G12 remain separate tracks under [`STATUS`](STATUS.md) and the active
platform configs. In particular, do not transfer G12 root/contact or thermal
results to the fixed-fin G80 candidate, or treat G80 prioritization as completion
of aggregate WO-03.
