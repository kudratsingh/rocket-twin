## Force balance and mass ownership to document

F1 removes the A1 removable aft joint, but still needs real internal attachments. The [manufacturer examples](wo03-g80-fixed-construction-references.md) lines 9–14 show a **topology precedent**: cord eye at the forward motor centering ring, bonded ring to motor tube, bonded mount into slotted body and TTW fin roots. Their kit eyes, knots, glue and paper joints supply no F1 capacity. The positive nose route and forward electronics/body retention must be explicit, not inferred from an ASA shoulder or a part-parent link.

Draw a free body at each **real** line, knot/eye, canopy shroud, nose branch, upper body/equipment branch, forward centering-ring eye, ring/tube bond, motor tube, aft ring/TTW fin/body transfer and motor retainer. For any chosen cut, assign each physical mass once to the side it follows at that stage of deployment; split long tubes and distributed harness by actual bounds, and keep the 50 g allowance once in a conservative location. Separate canopy-side mass from body-side mass. The sum of candidate section masses must reconcile to the one complete F1 mass, with any straddling or soft-goods ownership labelled unresolved rather than silently assigned by point CG. The [older branch ledger](wo03-g80-recovery-branch-loads.md) is a method example, not F1 mass allocation.

For a serial single-path *screen*, the idealized demand at a section can be written `T_cut,screen = 3 m_downstream (v²/(2s)+g)` under the stated collinear, common-deceleration assumptions. For an actual multi-branch event, vector force balance is `ΣT_i + F_aero + m g = m a` for each retained group with its measured/modeled acceleration and branch direction. A sum that balances on paper does not determine each `T_i`; branch stiffness, geometry, engagement order and motion decide that. Do not claim one third of the full load per fin or ring, and do not assume the ring-eye force is carried by only the motor tube while the **nominal 0.9525 mm F1 kraft wall** (from 66.675/64.77 mm OD/ID), upper equipment and nose are unrestrained. The motor must remain positively retained in the body in a recovery event; its recovery inertial path is distinct from the separate **450 N A1 motor thrust/reverse screen** and must not be capped by it.

## Feasible owner evidence sequence after a coherent F1 design

1. **Document and weigh** the exact F1 canopy, all shrouds, packed nose, body, motor/retainer, complete line/knots/eyes/protection, avionics cell and attachment hardware. Record where each item moves during separation; measure actual slack, free length, stretch and available stroke at the chosen pack. Check the exact G80 motor/retainer drawing, delay/charge identification and independent electronics revision before claiming a complete event sequence.
2. **Inspect representative assemblies** for end access, body/eye bearing, knot/sewn-eye geometry, chafe, heat protection, fin/root/ring bond continuity and retention after packing cycles. Perform controlled conditioned static pull tests on the *installed* eye/ring/tube/body/fin and nose attachment paths at the selected screening demands, recording load-displacement and failure mode. A cord-only catalogue break load does not test the assembly.
3. **Measure the event mechanisms separately** with appropriately instrumented, remotely operated ground fixtures: controlled separation/deployment at realistic packed geometry, then a contained dummy-mass line-extension test with load cell and synchronized motion/high-speed video for slack take-up and inflation/line stretch. Vary plausible line slack, temperature/moisture condition and asymmetric branch engagement. Use representative energy and appropriate range/safety procedures; do not infer peak from a benign low-speed pull.
4. **Integrate and close** only after source-pinned F1 flight timing, same-configuration mass/force histories and completed installed proof tests agree with the selected criterion. Preserve a failing or unavailable transient as such. Owner-controlled physical build, live deployment and flight remain later gates, not consequences of this review.

**Stop condition for this review:** if F1 has no selected canopy `CdS`, actual line/stroke and attachments, exact current G80/backup timing, complete mass or defensible branch free bodies, report the inflation and snatch peaks **unavailable**. Continue the retained 5 m/s and 20.4 m/s/0.3 m/DF3 comparisons; do not select a weaker criterion or promote kit precedent and smooth JAX inflation into qualification.
