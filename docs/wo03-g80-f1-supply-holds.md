# G80-F1 one-piece tube and motor-fit supply hold

**Decision, 2026-11-02 local:** The corrected G80-F1 body is **one continuous kraft tube, 1,060 mm long, 66.675 mm OD and 64.77 mm ID**. The bounded supplier check did not find a currently listed one-piece tube with all three specified dimensions. The body-stock gate is therefore **hold**, not a presumption that a 2.6 in or LOC 2.63 in kit tube is interchangeable. The current G80-10T SKU 78010 to retainer/mount fit remains a separate hold. This amendment changes no candidate geometry, source config, analysis result or A1 decision.

The amendment used **three new unique supplier technical URLs attempted** (two readable, one timed out) under the four-URL and five-minute caps. Search-result discovery and prior source rereads did not count. No purchasing, contact, physical measurement, CAD, simulation, or browser/computer use occurred. All page checks are 2026-11-02 local.

**Next bounded supplier input/stop:** Identify an actually purchasable single kraft blank at least 1,060 mm long with controlled **66.675 mm OD and 64.77 mm ID** (including tolerances, straightness, wall and cut length), or explicitly revise G80-F1 body geometry and recheck every dependent mass, nose/fin/ring fit, CP/CG, guide, thermal and recovery interface. Until both are resolved, stop before whole-vehicle CAD admission; neither a same-nominal-diameter kit nor a two-piece tube silently clears the one-piece requirement.
