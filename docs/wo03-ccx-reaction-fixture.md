## Original input preparation (historical)

All cases comprise three separate vertical, fully bottom-restrained bars centered at x = −10, 0 and +10 mm. Each is 20 mm long, has Poisson ratio zero, and receives distributed axial end loading. Step 1 applies `[50, 50, 50] N`; step 2 resets the loads and applies `[150, 0, 0] N`. Each step has total applied force `[0, 0, 150] N`; the independent exact moments about the origin are `[0, 0, 0]` and `[0, 1500, 0] N·mm`. There are no explicit MPC, equation or transform cards. Both all-node and support-node `RF` blocks are requested under distinct set names; the existing parser retains them under distinct keys, as a synthetic label-separation test confirms.

C's exact CAD annulus area is 21.2057504 mm², while its curved quadratic end meshes integrate to about 21.1881–21.1885 mm². C's integrated equal-step moment was `[0.0183054, −0.0418242, 0] N·mm`, not the exact zero moment; its single-step moment was `[0.0194609, 1499.94126, 0] N·mm`. The unchanged 1e-6 N·mm input check rejected C. The cause has not been isolated beyond the discrete end-face resultant; no correction, tolerance relaxation or native C run was made. An affine moment-equilibrating traction would alter the uniform-traction assumption and exact displacement reference, and therefore needs a separate explicit decision.

The proposed native packet is at most three serial cases, each with 5,000-node and 5,000-tetrahedron caps and **10 CPU s / 30 s wall** per case; stop at the first solver, malformed-output, force or displacement failure. The existing reaction checker requires `|residual| + print-rounding bound ≤ 1e-5 N` for a pass; a merely possible zero inside the printed interval is **unavailable**, not a pass. Loaded-bar top-node displacement error must be at most 1% of the exact reference; the unloaded-bar absolute limit is 1e-8 mm. Applied and support moments are reported independently with native rounding bounds; this reader does not introduce an acceptance threshold for moment. These are numerical fixture checks, not material or installed-strength criteria. A/B native cases must be explicitly declared by the primary before any invocation; C remains unavailable until its input discrepancy is resolved.

CalculiX completed both steps of A with no warning lines. Its printed top-node displacements exactly matched the stated references at the displayed precision: 0.0625 mm for each loaded bar in step 1, and 0.1875 / 0 / 0 mm in step 2. Thus their **reported** absolute and relative errors were zero; the native print precision limits what that establishes. The axial component alone has rounding bounds of **1.2e-5 N** in step 1 and **5.3e-5 N** in step 2; including transverse components cannot reduce the norm. Therefore both strict force outcomes are **unavailable due to print precision**, not passed and not a demonstrated numerical equilibrium failure. The packet stopped after A as predeclared. **B was not run**, and C remains unavailable from the earlier input-moment failure. No tolerance or output precision was changed, and no retry was performed.

## Separately authorized B-only material-contrast diagnostic

After preserving the original A precision stop, the primary explicitly selected **one B-only diagnostic** to investigate whether a gross reaction deficit appears in the simple conformal E=100/10,000 MPa layered bars. The earlier A/B packet remains closed with B unrun; this separate [B record](../build/wo03-ccx-reaction-native-b-diagnostic-v1/record.json) does not revise it. The unchanged saved v6 B inputs were copied without remeshing. The fixture generator and pinned CalculiX binary matched, and all sources and input files remained unchanged from packet admission through execution. As with A, the v6 generation had not recorded original deck/metadata hashes, so the packet does not claim historical hash verification before admission.

CalculiX completed both steps without warning lines. Support axial `RF` summed to approximately −150 N in each step; the observed force-residual norms were **1.248e-11 N** and **1.314e-11 N**, far smaller than the G80 bridge's 104.7 N equal-load discrepancy. The separately labeled all-node `RF` axial sums were approximately **−3.19e-11 N** and **−6.14e-11 N**. The applied/support moment residual vectors were approximately `[2.02e-11, −5.58e-11, 2.02e-11]` and `[1.72e-11, −5.87e-11, 9.92e-11] N·mm`; these are reported, with their print-rounding bounds retained in the record, without an invented moment criterion.

At displayed precision, the equal-step top displacements matched 0.315625 mm per bar with maximum absolute error **5.55e-17 mm** (relative **1.76e-16**); the single-step values matched 0.946875 / 0 / 0 mm with reported zero absolute error. These are printed-value comparisons, not infinite-precision measurements.

The unchanged strict force criterion is still **unavailable due to print precision** for both steps: the axial reaction-rounding bounds alone were **1.2e-5 N** and **5.3e-5 N**, each larger than the 1e-5 N criterion. No force tolerance was widened, and neither strict result is a pass. The B child used **0.083818 CPU s**, **0.112606 s wall**, and a largest sampled RSS of **11,632,640 bytes**; eleven fixture/control tests passed before execution. Some older setup CPU remains unavailable. This result shows no gross discrepancy at printed resolution in the simple material-contrast case; it does not identify the bridge discrepancy, qualify the actual method, or establish structural acceptance. No A rerun, C/C2 case, bridge mesh, retry, or further native case occurred.
