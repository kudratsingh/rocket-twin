front edges250/430mm from the nose. For the current1227mm candidate tail and
an explicitly assumed100mm pad offset, the upper lug starts1077mm above the
clear guide bottom. Conservatively ending guidance at its first departure gives:

`usable travel = measured clear overall length −1.077m`.

Thus a hypothetical clear10ft/3.048m guide gives1.971m usable. This is an
illustration, **not confirmed equipment**. The actual button head/slot/end,
stop, blast deflector, stand-off, occupied rail and release geometry can change
