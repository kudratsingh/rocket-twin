## Force path and realistic boundary conditions

For an axial recovery test, apply force through the actual NW-12/leader
junction, not directly to a fictitious rigid annulus. React through a compliant
split body saddle and, in a separately measured case, a separate mount-tube
reaction fixture. Instrument each reaction if both are used; their sum must
balance applied force within the calibrated uncertainty. A fore-body-only grip
forces the full load through one path and is a **conservative characterization
configuration**, not a solved flight load split. If the grip crushes/slips or
adds an unrecorded stiffener, that test does not establish the intended joint.

Use axial pull first, then defined off-axis line angles0/15/30deg in the notch
plane and between fins, and slack-branch seating. These are proposed initial
test orientations, not a demonstrated worst-case recovery envelope. Measure
ring movement, tube ovalization, body deformation, knot/tail migration and
failure location. One taut branch can take force before another; no50/50 split.
Retain severe whole2014.309N/body1400.931N demand screens for55g adverse
(or recompute at as-built mass); hypothetical68g whole2041.742N is not a mass
bound. A stated static load is not the deployment peak or a proof that a slow
pull covers shock loading. Do not multiplyDF3 again.

For a separate installed-fin experiment use the actual distributed aerodynamic
equivalent or a characterized loading yoke:8.83N·m, transverse directions both
signs, with declared331.125N resultant. A single tip force147.167N at60mm
matches moment **but not shear distribution** and must be a separate diagnostic.
Measure root rotation relative to body and mount, panel movement and support
flexibility. The optimistic panel already fails; do not call a fixture that
constrains it evidence for the installed design. Modal/installed flutter and
validated operating envelope remain a later design/test gate.

## Proposed stages and acceptance meaning

1. **Nonloaded construction/dry-fit:** label exact products/lot; weigh raw and
   finished assemblies; measure dressed topology, tail lengths, actual loop
   bearing length, gap/ovality, knot box and accessible reach. Pass only if all
   stated geometric bounds and0.1mm applicable clearances are met without
   trimming required tails or moving supports. Inspect before permanent closure.
2. **Finished-end characterization:** calibrated force–extension, unloading,
   seating/slip and failure location, with force rate/gauge length recorded.
   No published minimum finished rating exists. A single success is not a
   qualified allowable; the owner-approved protocol must define replication,
   environmental/statistical basis and acceptance before a flight claim.
3. **Installed load path:** cold/dry baseline, then justified hot/wet material
   conditions and bond/cure variants. Keep45°C substrate screen; record actual
   component temperatures rather than an oven setpoint alone. Assess crush,
   ring fracture, peel, tube/body failure, knot slip and repeated flex/chafe.
   Damaged or failed specimens stay failed, not retested as independent samples.
4. **Thermal/packing/deployment evidence later:** instrument burn/soak through
   existing required cases on the eventual admitted motor/shield assembly;
   verify sleeve ends, bare passage, mouth, knot and electronics. Ground
   deployment/packing tests need their separate owner protocol, safe facilities
   and release. A storyboard or slow static pull cannot replace those tests.
