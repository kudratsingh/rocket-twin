## Birch stock and property fork

The US supplier [Aircraft Spruce Finnish Birch 02-20850](https://www.aircraftspruce.com/catalog/pnpages/02-20850.php) lists a **24 × 48 in half-sheet, nominal 6 mm, 12 plies**, $69.75, Add to Cart, face grain along its 48 in cut direction and seller-estimated sheet weight **0.92 lb/ft²**. This is a concrete 6 mm cut-stock candidate. The page does not state a controlled thickness tolerance, product-specific along/across bending or planar-shear characteristic values, 45 °C or motor-adjacent conditioned properties, or cut/finished fin/ring mass. Its Q&A cites GL-2, but a general rulebook is not a batch certificate or directional material data. No mass/strength from a different birch product is transferred to 02-20850.

An alternative with a directly traceable manufacturer property declaration is [Metsä Wood Birch PF 6.5 mm](https://www.metsagroup.com/contentassets/2e9ddda886f04453825068d3b37afcdc/uk_doc_metsawood_birch_plywood_pf_2022-11-07.pdf). Its [manufacturer data sheet](https://www.metsagroup.com/contentassets/8a5519dc6e534fcfa99c06759fbb8bad/metsa_wood_birch_plywood_product_datasheets_en.pdf) gives nominal **6.5 mm, five plies, 6.1–6.9 mm thickness range and 4.4 kg/m²** at stated moisture conditions; phenol-formaldehyde bond, EN 314-2 Class 3. The UK conformity declaration gives at nominal 6.5 mm **characteristic bending strength 50.9 N/mm² along face grain and 29.0 across**, **mean bending modulus 12,737/4,763 N/mm²**, and **characteristic planar shear 3.20/1.78 N/mm²** along/across. Those are declared **construction-product** values for that identified stock, not fin-panel hot allowables. [DHH Panel Products](https://www.dhhpanelproducts.co.uk/product/metsa-birch-plywood/) lists its 6.5 mm BB/BB exterior sheet as typically ex-stock in the UK, with no public checkout price or confirmed US delivery. It does not supply a 45 °C derating, local motor-hot proof, cut-part mass, impact or flutter qualification.

**Selection boundary:** F2 must use either exact 02-20850 with its missing directional/hot data retained as a hold, or deliberately adopt the 6.5 mm Metsä item, update all fin/ring geometry and mass and verify procurement in the owner's region. The stronger Metsä DoC cannot be used to fill 02-20850 fields. A nominal thickness and panel strength alone do not establish through-the-wall root, ring-eye or paper-body joint capacity.

### Separately bounded Eggtimer alternative check

The [Eggtimer Apogee user guide](https://eggtimerrocketry.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Eggtimer-Apogee-Users-Guide.pdf) identifies a **single apogee/nose-over deployment channel**, a **1.75 × 0.9 in, about 5 g board**, built-in screw power switch, JST-PH input for a **1S 3.7 V LiPo**, and four #4 mounting screws. It forbids 2S or 9 V power and instructs polarity checking for other JST-PH packs. Its barometric port needs venting. The [manufacturer price/order page](https://eggtimerrocketry.com/purchase/), marked 3 June 2026, lists the Apogee kit at **$15**, a **$34 starter package** containing Apogee, 110 mAh 1S cell and charger, and **$10 ground shipping** for shipments with a LiPo. The battery is not sold separately. The vendor requires an email and invoice process and publishes no live stock count; **actual availability is unconfirmed**. No outreach was made. As a kit, it requires owner assembly and a separate ground/functional verification. Its one channel cannot silently replace a two-channel F2 deployment sequence.

The [Eggtimer Quark product page](https://eggtimerrocketry.com/eggtimer-quark/) describes a two-channel drogue/main controller, **44 × 17.75 mm, about 7 g**, accepting **6–15 V** with **2S 7.4 V LiPo preferred**; it is a solder-assembly kit. The same price list gives **$20 basic / $25 Easy Kit** and an optional $5 USB-serial test interface, before battery, switch, charging, wiring and shipping. Its [current support page](https://eggtimerrocketry.com/eggtimer-quark-support/) links D3–D6 assembly manuals, D3+ user guide and D3–D6 dimension drawing, but these PDFs were not opened within the four-URL amendment. The prior 120 mAh **1S** recovery cell is below Quark's published 6 V minimum and cannot be assigned to it. Quark's exact current delivered revision, 2S cell stock/mass/connector, switch, full height and mounting hole pattern and live kit stock are unresolved.

**Controller conclusion:** Apogee offers a source-backed small one-channel path with a listed matching cell/charger package; Quark offers two channels but needs a different battery and more parts. Neither is selected as the F2 installed controller. The primary must choose the deployment sequence first, then source one exact available revision and power/arming/output assembly and check its completed mass and occupancy. The Eggtimer seller's email-only process prevents confirming live stock under the no-contact instruction.
