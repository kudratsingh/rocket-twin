## Response window

Record each inquiry independently. Do not infer a send date from a file timestamp,
publication, permission to prepare a message, or another recipient's send date.
Until an actual send is supplied, its status is **send date not recorded**, with
no response deadline or overdue finding. The original five drafts remain the
frozen UNSENT source records; later correspondence belongs in a separate record.

The actual send date is day 0. Schedule one follow-up after five business days
and close the response window after ten business days. The tracker must state
its weekday/holiday calendar and time zone; weekday-only estimates must not
pretend to account for supplier closures. There is no automatic sending or
background scheduler. The owner supplies correspondence; agents prepare any
follow-up text without sending it.

Use the blank [response tracker](validation/rp1-vp1/response-tracker.json),
[decision template](validation/rp1-vp1/response-decision-template.md) and the
read-only `scripts/wo03_vp1_inquiry_tracker.py` helper. Keep actual correspondence
and quotes in an ignored local `.cache/vp1-correspondence/` copy; publish the
sanitized engineering decision and permitted source facts. This public template
does not become a sent-message record merely because the owner sends an inquiry.

Close the documentation round on schedule, even if a supplier cannot provide
conditioned properties or finished-joint data. Missing supplier information is
not a demonstrated material failure. A reply describing typical raw-material
properties cannot establish the strength or flexibility of our installed root.

## One proposed first round at room temperature

Reconcile the replies into one concrete owner-review decision. Include:

1. The exact material and adhesive/process identity, stock tolerance and source
   for each claim. Ordinary birch/WEST 105/206/406 remains the first investigation
   lead, not an adopted fin or a reason to buy every alternative.
2. The laboratory's proposed specimen, fixture, force/displacement method,
   calibration, resolution, uncertainty, replication, conditioning and reporting.
   Check these against the existing VP1 protocol before calling the method valid.
3. An itemized total covering consumed samples, adhesive/preparation, reusable
   fixtures/tools, calibrated channels, setup/labour, reporting, shipping and taxes
   where applicable. Mark estimates, exclusions and quote validity. An unquoted
   item keeps the all-in total unavailable; the historical stock subtotal and
   internal planning reservation are not the total or spending authorization.
4. What a pass, failure or inconclusive result establishes. Room-temperature
   strips and joint witnesses can screen a material/process and inform the next
   root decision. They cannot establish hot/wet properties, installed-root
   movement, recovery capacity, thermal limits, flutter or flight readiness.
5. The exact purchase/testing authorization requested, who would perform it,
   proposed expenditure, stop conditions and the next decision it enables.

Keep D1's 8.83 N·m / 331.125 N once-factored demand, 1 mm total movement and existing
estimated material screens unchanged. A later complete-root measurement replaces
its modeled contribution rather than being added twice. The separate S2b root
hold and supported-construction/method/quote/owner-authorization prerequisites
remain. No new fin variant, detailed CAD/FEA or stopped numerical retry follows
from a supplier reply alone.
