### Finite overlap and verification

Each paper adherend has independent axial displacement, radial displacement and rotation. The 0.1 mm film has distributed shear and normal elastic springs over the actual finite 100 mm overlap. Surface offsets and finite-film rotation terms retain eccentricity-induced peel and make a rigid rotation strain-free. Only the base tube is supported, 50 mm ahead of the overlap. No end of the outer paper sleeve is grounded, tied to the support or projected into the forward joint. Independent compressive end forces bracket 0%, 28.57% and 100% of the load introduced through the outer paper; the middle value is an estimated equal-strain split, not a demonstrated shoulder-contact solution. The local displacement excludes the rest of the 778 mm upper tube.

The formulation minimizes axial, bending, adhesive shear and normal strain energy. It is a small-rotation beam/elastic-foundation reduction, following the bonded-adherend approach of Goland and Reissner, *Journal of Applied Mechanics* 11 (1944), A17–A27; Euler-Bernoulli interpolation follows Gere, *Mechanics of Materials*. The optional axisymmetric cylindrical-shell hoop foundation follows circumferential strain `u_r/R` as in Timoshenko and Woinowsky-Krieger, *Theory of Plates and Shells*. Both adherends use a common thin-wall circumference at R33; their actual annular masses are calculated separately. Anisotropy, finite rotations, shell ovalisation/non-axisymmetric modes, failure, contact, defects, thermal strain and fracture are not modeled.

Three levels use 120/240/480 divisions, with the overlap start on an exact node. An equal-EA Volkersen axial shear-lag fixture agrees with its closed-form traction field to maximum relative errors 1.657×10⁻⁵, 4.167×10⁻⁶ and 1.045×10⁻⁶. An independent double-cantilever normal-foundation fixture checks peel stiffness against the semi-infinite end-opening solution `w(0)=P/(2Dβ³)`, `β=(k/4D)^(1/4)`, following Hetenyi, *Beams on Elastic Foundation* (1946). Its errors are 6.255×10⁻⁷, 3.920×10⁻⁸ and 2.607×10⁻⁹; the fixture is more than 13 decay lengths long. Both fixtures pass equilibrium/strain-energy checks. The flat-beam rigid-rotation relative stiffness residual is at most 1.1×10⁻¹⁶. Candidate axial equilibrium residual is below 1.1×10⁻⁸ N.
