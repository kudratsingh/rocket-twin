# CalculiX reaction fixture D: Poisson coupling preparation

**Current outcome:** D completed once; its printed displacements meet the analytic criterion, while strict force certification remains unavailable due to output precision. It is not a bridge or installed-strength pass.

## Original preparation (historical)

Fixture D was initially prepared without native execution. It reuses the admitted case-B mesh and loads to isolate nonzero Poisson coupling with an exact uniaxial-strain reference. It is a proposed small numerical diagnostic, not an actual bridge or installed-strength analysis.

The [prepared deck](../build/wo03-ccx-poisson-diagnostic-v1/case.inp) is derived from the saved B deck with exactly three reversible byte edits: each of the two elastic material definitions changes `ν=0` to `ν=0.3`, and `ALL,1,2,0` is added to the existing boundary block. The original `SUPPORT,1,3,0` restraint is retained. Reversing those edits restores B's deck byte-for-byte; node positions, connectivity, moduli, material ownership, two `OP=NEW` load steps and output requests are unchanged. B's input hashes match its previously admitted diagnostic record and remained unchanged during D preparation. The 593-node, 254-tetrahedron input remains below the existing 5,000/5,000 caps.

All three independent 4 × 4 mm bars have two conformal 10 mm axial layers, E = 100 / 10,000 MPa, and ν = 0.3. Constraining x/y displacement everywhere makes `εxx = εyy = 0`; for isotropic linear elasticity, the effective axial modulus is

`Eeff = E (1 − ν) / [(1 + ν)(1 − 2ν)] = 1.346153846153846 E`.

For 50 N / 150 N on a bar, `σzz = F / 16 mm²` is 3.125 / 9.375 MPa and `σxx = σyy = ν σzz / (1 − ν)` is 1.339285714 / 4.017857143 MPa in both layers. Direct Lamé-parameter substitution independently reproduces this stress tensor for each modulus. The equal Poisson ratios give continuous interface displacement and axial traction. Integrating the layer strains gives exact top `Uz` of **0.234464285714 mm** for 50 N and **0.703392857143 mm** for 150 N. Step 1 loads all bars at 50 N; step 2 loads the first at 150 N and the remaining two at zero. The printed deck's independently recomputed total applied forces are 150 N in both steps, with moments approximately `[0, 0, 0]` and `[0, 1500, 0] N·mm`.

Unlike the earlier ν=0 fixture, the bottom support set is **not the complete set of constrained degrees of freedom**: every node is also constrained in x/y. Under the declared no-DLOAD/no-MPC model, the planned reader uses labeled `ALL RF` and subtracts the known serialized nodal CLOAD at each node to reconstruct total reaction. It then adds the applied load back when evaluating overall force/moment balance; equivalently the complete `ALL RF` sum represents the balance. `SUPPORT RF` Z is separately checked against −150 N. Using support-set x/y forces alone as total reaction would omit lateral constraints. The reader retains the existing **1e-5 N strict force criterion including the conservative printed-value rounding bound**; an interval that merely permits equilibrium is unavailable, not a pass. Per-bar axial displacement errors are checked against 1% of the loaded top reference, with 1e-8 mm for unloaded bars and zero transverse displacement; all-node coverage is required. Moment residuals and print-rounding bounds are reported without inventing an acceptance threshold.

The [preflight record](../build/wo03-ccx-poisson-diagnostic-v1/preflight.json) pins the source B admission record, all three B inputs, the prepared deck and preparation/reader source hashes. Seven focused no-solver tests passed, including the tensor substitution, exact reversible byte changes, parser coverage, and a synthetic example proving applied CLOAD is subtracted before total-reaction evaluation. Preparation used **0.118923 CPU s**, **0.126324 s wall**, and **40,108,032 bytes** child peak RSS; its hard child cap was 10 CPU s / 30 s wall. No mesh regeneration or solver run occurred.

If the primary separately declares the native case, D would consume one case at 10 CPU s / 30 s wall, one worker requested, as the **third and final slot** in the original three-native-case diagnostic limit (A and B already used two). Earlier setup CPU that was not measured remains unavailable, not zero; the unchanged 600 CPU s overall limit still applies. Any output, accuracy, resource or parser failure stops that case. No precision-unavailable result qualifies the method, and even a numerical pass would not close the G80 bridge load path or structural acceptance.

## One authorized native D case: strict force precision unavailable

The primary subsequently authorized one D case as the third and final native fixture slot. The preserved D input and metadata were copied without remeshing to a fresh [native execution record](../build/wo03-ccx-poisson-native-d-v1/record.json); this explicit authorization overrides the earlier preparation-only flag for D alone. The copied deck matched the prepared D deck and its reversible B edits; original B admission inputs, D preparation sources, solver binary and before/after source hashes remained unchanged. Fourteen focused parser, classification and owned-process tests passed before execution. No A, B, C, thermal or actual-bridge case was rerun.

CalculiX completed both steps with no warning lines. All-node axial displacement comparisons against the piecewise uniaxial-strain reference met the declared 1% threshold: the largest printed absolute error on each loaded bar was approximately **5.0e-8 mm**. Transverse displacements were reported as zero; the unloaded bars' reported axial errors were zero. These comparisons use the printed native values and do not imply infinite precision.

`ALL RF` minus the known deck CLOAD produced observed total force-residual norms of **1.448e-11 N** and **1.161e-10 N** for the equal and single steps. Their componentwise conservative output-rounding bounds were `[8.7e-5, 8.7e-5, 2.4e-5] N` and `[3.98e-4, 3.98e-4, 2.78e-4] N`. Bottom `SUPPORT RF` Z separately summed to approximately **−150 N** in each step, with rounding bounds **1.2e-5 / 5.3e-5 N**. Consequently, the unchanged **1e-5 N** strict checks are **unavailable due to print precision**, not passed; neither observed discrepancy is a gross force imbalance at the printed resolution. The moment residual vectors were approximately `[−1.46e-10, 3.42e-10, 1.01e-11]` and `[−1.39e-10, 8.66e-10, −8.94e-11] N·mm`; the propagated print bounds are recorded, and no moment threshold is invented.

The solver's allocation summary lists **1,303 single-point constraints**, **one MPC with one term**, and zero tie constraints **as estimated upper bounds**, not as observed actual constraint counts. The deck prescribes x/y on all 593 nodes plus x/y/z on 39 bottom nodes: that is 1,303 input node-DOF entries but **1,225 unique node-DOF pairs**, with 78 identical x/y overlaps at the bottom. Both assembled steps report **554 matrix equations**, exactly `3 × 593 − 1,225`. This is consistent with the unique-constraint arithmetic, but does not prove internal implementation details. The estimated MPC upper bound does not establish an actual hidden MPC or its effect. These facts do not alter the observed output classification.

The direct child used **0.066246 CPU s**, **0.118181 s wall**, and a largest sampled RSS of **106,856,448 bytes**, under the declared 10 CPU s / 30 s wall limits with one worker requested. Some earlier setup CPU remains unavailable, not zero. At the available printed resolution, this simple, constrained material-contrast Poisson case does not reproduce the bridge's roughly 104.7 N equal-load discrepancy. It does not prove the strict 1e-5 N balance, identify the bridge discrepancy, validate a curved bridge/support configuration or qualify installed strength. The original three native fixture slots are now used; no further fixture, mesh tuning, or bridge run follows automatically.
