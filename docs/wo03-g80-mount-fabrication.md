# G80 fixed mount: proposed buildable assembly and open joints

The selected **52-part G80 proposal is not yet a buildable, qualified mount**. Its `motor_mount` CAD is a fused plywood solid, while the three aft retainer blocks are actually at **60°, 180° and 300° between the 0°, 120° and 240° rails**. Each block begins at the aft face of the last 3 mm ring; the current fused join is an axial butt connection to that ring, not continuous rail material. The single immediate correction proposed for bounded CAD checking is a **slotted G11 aft bridge with six rail-flank tabs and three through-bolt positive-capture paths**, with finite block, ring and rail joints and explicit mass accounting before another structural solve. Its geometry, contact and capacity are not yet established. This is a proposed route for the existing G80 candidate, not adoption or a new vehicle family.

The selected source is the frozen 52-part integrated identity `62e8de9eae802300bd36e5bdfbd08c8d20635d138972bb07708f574103ed7009` and its [resolved copy](../build/wo03-g80-integrated-candidate-v1/resolved-candidate.yaml). The [fit report](wo03-g80-fit-candidate.md) and [load-path report](wo03-g80-mount-loadpath.md) supply its 32.2 mm radial trim and existing conditional screens. The dimensions below are owned by the companion [proposed study configuration](../configs/studies/wo03-g80-mount-fabrication.yaml); they are **not yet CAD or a reconciled mass model**.

## One immediate bridge correction to check

Propose a G11 annular bridge at local **170.1–172.1 mm**, radii **15.8–32.2 mm**, with a 0.1 mm gap to the last plywood ring at 170 mm. Provide three rail notches, width 12.2 mm and inner radius 24.9 mm, and six integral G11 tabs beside the rails at local **172.1–187.1 mm**. Each tab starts with a 7 mm radial × 2 mm tangential section and nominal 0.1 mm rail-flank gap, trimmed to R32.2; retain the existing fin channels and M3 bore axes; relocate the nuts to the G11 bridge's forward face as described below. The retainer blocks begin at **172.2 mm** and end at 190 mm, with a nominal 0.1 mm block-to-bridge gap. This geometry is a defined single candidate for inspection, not proof it fits or transmits load.

At existing estimated G11 density **1850 kg/m³**, the full uncut 2 mm annulus is **9.150 g** and six untrimmed 7 × 2 × 15 mm tabs are **2.331 g**, a combined **11.481 g gross upper volume estimate** before notches, bores, clipping, joints or altered blocks. Shortening the three original blocks by 2.2 mm removes approximately **0.637 g** of plywood if the nominal 3.4 mm bores are the only material cuts in that slice; exact net mass, CG, inertia and adhesive allocation need CAD reconciliation. The bridge requires three longer replacement screws and new or demonstrably suitable nuts, washers and keepers; their added mass is not established. Six ideal rail-flank projections, using `sqrt(32.2²−6.1²)−25` radial width and 15 mm axial length, give approximately **595.52 mm²**. If they alone share all 117.53 N uniformly, the DF3 shear demand is **0.592 MPa** versus the existing estimated 1 MPa hot shear screen. This is only an upper-area idealization: actual tab root continuity, clipping, uneven load, normal/peel, G11 plate bending and rail/shell reaction remain unknown. The intended through-bolts bypass block butt tension; local G11 bending, bearing and nut capture still require verification.

A bounded geometry check must confirm that the three screws and forward captive nuts/washers bear on the bridge and that its tabs connect to six rail flanks without collision with fin tabs, channel cuts, shell, plate, nozzle or assembly paths. Until then the 11.481 g estimate is neither a reconciled candidate mass nor evidence that the construction is feasible. G11 and epoxy hot properties are only existing estimates; the wood/paper substrate's 45°C provisional screen and qualified thermal method remain separate open dependencies.

## Through-bolt capture: proposed stack and required relief

The bridge forward face is at local **170.1 mm** and the retainer-cap aft face at **197 mm**. At each unchanged bolt axis, specify an exploratory **3.4 mm bridge bore**, an **8 mm OD × 3.4 mm ID × 1 mm washer envelope** from 169.1–170.1 mm, and a **6.6 × 6.6 × 3 mm nut envelope** from 166.1–169.1 mm. These are geometry envelopes for the next check, not selected supplier hardware or proved ratings. A keeper or keyed feature must provide actual nut retention and anti-rotation without blocking installation; its geometry, material and mass are unknown.

The existing last plywood ring occupies local **167–170 mm**, leaving only 0.1 mm between it and the bridge. The washer and nut therefore cannot occupy the proposed locations without a new relief. One bounded next CAD check should cut three radial-open reliefs at the bolt angles: radial **21.5–34.5 mm**, tangential width **8.2 mm**, axial **166.1–170.1 mm**, clipped by the actual ring. This removes material through the full 3 mm plywood ring and extends 0.9 mm ahead of it. The nominal inner radial ligament is **21.5−15.8 = 5.7 mm** before adjacent rail pockets, bore tolerances and local stress. The relief is a potential structural and assembly conflict, not a validated solution: check separation from 0°/120°/240° rail pockets, fin channels, shell, nut insertion and keeper access before any capture claim.

For an ideal 8 mm washer and 3.4 mm hole, projected annular area is **41.186 mm² per seat**. Equal sharing of the 150 N reverse demand gives **50 N** per seat and **3.642 MPa** mean bearing after DF3. The existing nominal M3 tensile-area assumption 5.03 mm² gives **29.82 MPa** factored screw tensile demand. These are demands only: actual washer OD/bore, fastener grade, thread engagement/protrusion, washer flexure, G11 through-thickness bearing/pull-through, local 2 mm bridge bending, nut capture, tab load sharing and temperature-dependent allowables remain unavailable. Geometry from the nominal cap aft face to the nut forward face is **30.9 mm**; a screw must span at least that distance before allowance for protrusion, head seating and tolerance stack. No exact length is selected, and the existing 20 mm screw cannot be assumed adequate.

## Interface forces and conditional checks

The forward route is motor shoulder → mica compression pads → G11 plate → finite finger/rail and plate/shell joints → continuous rail blanks, rings and their finite bonds → shell and forward joint. The already checked 117.53 N source thrust and factor 3 apply once; actual ring load split and shell restraint remain unknown. The existing estimated **1 MPa hot adhesive shear** screen is not a measured wood/paper allowable.

At one ring, a simple geometric upper idealization for the three ring/rail pocket interfaces uses six side bands, each 3 mm axial thickness by `sqrt(32.2²−6²)−25 ≈ 6.636 mm` radial extent, plus three inner-end bands each 12 × 3 mm: approximately **227.45 mm²** total before real gap, slots, fillets, edge damage and coverage losses. If **all** 117.53 N were forced through just that one ring and shared uniformly, DF3 shear demand would be **1.550 MPa**, exceeding the estimated 1 MPa screen. Equal sharing among all three rings would give approximately **0.517 MPa** by the same arithmetic. Neither loading distribution has been solved; the two values are conditional sensitivities, not a failure or pass of the complete joint. The 0.1 mm pocket clearance does not itself guarantee full adhesion or equal load.

The reverse path is motor aft shoulder → aft mica pads/cap → three longer through-bolts → captive nuts and washers bearing directly on the **forward G11 bridge face** → G11 tabs beside the rails → rails/ring joints and shell → forward structure. The blocks serve as non-load-bearing guides/spacers for this tensile route. In the saved candidate, nuts are in the blocks and the block butt connects to the last plywood ring; the proposed positive-capture route is not already modeled. The existing reverse demand is **150 N total**, or 50 N per block under equal sharing. The nominal 12 × 12 mm butt footprint less one 3.4 mm bore is **134.921 mm² per block**, before imperfections or other cuts. If the old butt alone carried the demand uniformly, normal demand would be **0.371 MPa**, or **1.112 MPa after DF3**. That legacy load path is a normal/peel-sensitive joint and is not credited in the through-bolt proposal; the existing 1 MPa **shear** estimate cannot qualify it. Unequal screw load, prying, fastener preload, nut-pocket breakout, veneer splitting and joint tensile/peel strength are unknown. The proposed G11 bridge must carry the washer reactions into its tabs and rails. Its hypothetical washer seats, fastener capture, tab roots, bond normal/peel and end restraint require analysis and verification.

The prior net rail stress and hot Euler results assume intact longitudinal rails, ideal restraint at the three ring stations and the source effective section; they do not establish that adhesive-bonded ring pockets provide those restraints or transfer the required forces. Prior plate and finger sensitivities likewise assume support and wet bond. The aft-block butt joint and ring bending, interply shear/peel, shell transfer and mounted assembly stiffness have not been calculated or tested. Preserve those positive screens as **conditional** and do not assign them to a bonded subassembly without proving the required joints.

## Adhesive and fastener ledger

The source integrated mass includes a separate **4.6200 g** upper-budget annulus for the *incremental* R32.2–R32.3 gap over the full 190 mm. It is not a full baseline mount-shell bond, a measured wet area or a stiffness. It must be counted once. The source also has **12 g `adhesive_finish`** as an unresolved distributed sealer/finish/joint allowance, **8 g `fin_bonds`** for tab/fillet/gusset bonds, and **7 g `retention_hardware`** for the existing three screws, nuts/washers and locking provision. The latter hardware and fin budgets are not reassigned to these new internal joints. The three original screws must be replaced by longer ones. Because the 7 g hardware allowance is not itemized, it is not assumed to cover the replacement set or keeper: provisionally retain 7 g and **add the full new longer-screw/nut/washer/keeper set mass, currently unknown**, until an exact-once itemized hardware ledger supports reallocation. The 52-part mass and flight evidence cannot be carried forward unchanged.

## Immediate correction and stop criteria

Generate and inspect the single proposed G11 bridge correction below, including finite ring/rail/block interfaces, stated nominal gaps, no positive-volume part overlaps, preserved motor/liner/fin channels, three replacement through-bolts, forward washer/nut seats and plywood-ring relief, and an itemized adhesive/hardware ledger. Check actual mating areas, tolerance extremes, ring ligament and joint stiffness/load sharing, especially the G11 bridge-to-rail-tab load path and its restraint by rails/shell. For the existing demands, apply the original **DF3** to credible transferred forces and compare shear **and normal/peel** to suitable, separately sourced hot material/joint criteria; prove reverse 150 N capture, including fasteners, nuts, ring and shell. A mean shear proxy cannot pass unknown normal capacity.

**Stop** if the cut geometry prevents assembly, any gap/coverage cannot be represented, adhesive or required hardware cannot be charged once, the minimum material/contact section is unavailable, or the needed temperature-dependent joint capacities/restraints cannot be justified. Record the missing dependency and do not assume a monolithic plywood joint or increase the screen. No CAD generation, FEA, thermal analysis, flight run, fabrication or adoption was performed in this definition; the primary retains the candidate decision and all other WO-03/physical gates remain open.
