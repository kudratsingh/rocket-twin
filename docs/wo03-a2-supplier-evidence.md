# A2 recovery-backbone and motor-interface supplier evidence

**Decision, 2026-11-02 local:** The current exact G80-10T SKU 78010 and Estes 009750 retainer do not have a public, dimensioned and mutually approved mating stack in the reviewed official material. A2 must keep the motor seat plane, nozzle/retainer sweep and liner termination unresolved. A1 remains frozen and stopped; no A2 detailed CAD/FEA or material, motor or retainer substitution is admitted by this research. A continuous recovery route is a credible construction topology, but no reviewed source supplies its finished hot/wet dynamic capacity in the proposed A2 geometry.

The [machine-readable register](../configs/studies/wo03-a2-supplier-evidence.yaml) records **10 new unique official manufacturer/technical URLs attempted**, nine readable and one timed out, under the 10-URL and 15-minute active-research caps. Search discovery, repeated reads of the current 78010 page and earlier A1/architecture URLs did not count as new. No browser/computer use, scripts, engineering solve, supplier contact, purchase or physical work occurred. These are dated live pages, without byte-pinned archives or a supplier revision agreement.

## Recovery route evidence

[Apogee's Level-2 assembly instructions](https://www.apogeerockets.com/downloads/PDFs/07230-Level-2-Instructions.pdf), accessed 2026-11-02, show a conventional 54 mm fiberglass motor tube bonded to a forward centering ring, with a forged eyebolt, washer and nut on each side of that ring and the lower shock cord tied to the eye. The instructions also put a second eye at the nose/bulkhead and call for later shock-cord connections. [Apogee's shock-cord anchoring article](https://www.apogeerockets.com/Peak-of-Flight/Newsletter573), accessed 2026-11-02, documents motor-tube/centering-ring routes and removable-anchor concepts. These sources support explicit motor-mount-to-body-to-upper connection topology and inspectable endpoints. They do **not** provide capacity for an A2 continuous backbone, finished flat webbing eye, bend/saddle, knot/seam, connector, ring/bond or body transfer under A2's hot/wet and asymmetric dynamic loads. The older [Samson warning](https://www.samsonrope.com/warning-statement) remains relevant: bend, termination and temperature alter system strength; raw rope ratings cannot be transferred to a finished A2 harness. No source-backed numerical finished-eye/bend capacity was found in this bounded pass.

The final four URL slots checked a lighter rocket recovery line. [Apogee's shock-cord comparison](https://apogeerockets.com/Building_Supplies/Parachutes_Recovery_Equipment/Shock_Cord) and its [750 lb Kevlar cord P/N 29507](https://www.apogeerockets.com/Building-Supplies/Shock-Cord/Kevlar-Cord-750) and [1500 lb Kevlar cord P/N 30327](https://www.apogeerockets.com/Building_Supplies/Parachutes_Recovery_Equipment/Shock_Cord/Kevlar_Cord_1500), accessed 2026-11-02, list nominal outer diameters **0.070 in** and **0.137 in**, respectively. Their storefronts list “Weight” **0.8 g** and **2 g** and say pricing is per foot. They do **not explicitly state those weight numbers are grams per foot**, distinguish stock-line mass from packaging/shipping mass, or give a measured finished-length mass. Accordingly the 2.5 m effective main backbone plus approximately 1.8 m upper and 0.6 m nose branches cannot receive a verified rope mass from them. Their raw “750 lb” and “1500 lb” cord claims also cannot establish eye, knot, seam, bend, chafe, hot/wet, dynamic or installed anchor capacity. The [Onebadhawk 3/16 in tubular Kevlar two-loop product](https://onebadhawk.com/316-tubular-kevlar--2-loop.html) timed out; its search snippet was not used for a mass or finished-loop claim. The earlier 12.7 mm flat Bally **17.36 g/m** remains a different format; substituting either round cord to meet mass would require an explicit geometry, contact/load, full-mass and recovery analysis. No lighter harness is selected by this source pass.

## Exact missing inputs and next bounded stop

For a source-informed A2 motor-interface drawing, obtain a controlled current **78010** drawing or record dimensions from later owner-supplied, unmodified, identified hardware: maximum/minimum case OD and roundness along insertion, assembled front-to-nozzle length and datum, thrust-ring OD/axial thickness/shoulder location/fillets, nozzle projection and contour, forward bulkhead/ejection clearance, case-to-mount contact restrictions and hot growth. For **009750**, obtain cap and fixed skirt ID/OD, internal lip/seat faces, thread form/engagement, compatible mount-tube OD/overhang/adhesive instructions, thermal limit and explicit 78010 pairing or a measured/approved fit. Record tolerances and lot/revision for both. Require a full removal/swept envelope after the retained barrier and independent recovery route are drawn. An unverified nominal diameter or a different DMS/phenolic case is a stop.

For the recovery route, identify the exact cord/webbing, **verified linear or cut-and-finished mass** including loops and chafe, eye/seam or knot, thread, connector, bend radius/contact width and every upper/aft anchor. Obtain product-specific finished-system conditioned test data or later owner-authorized representative assembly proof, including asymmetric and dynamic load and ring/body transfer. The smallest owner request after this search is therefore: **(1)** identify intended 78010 motor lot and 009750 retainer revision and supply controlled mating/installation drawings or later permit measurements of actual unmodified items; **(2)** identify the exact finished harness/eye/connector products, their measured mass per cut length or finished assembly mass, and manufacturer capacities at the intended bend and conditions, if any. Do not ask for broader catalogue work or physical testing before a complete A2 mass/function/occupancy screen. None of these supplier pages qualifies an installed joint or releases WO-03, G54/G12, guide-equipment or flight gates.
