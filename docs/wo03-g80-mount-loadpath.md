# G80 fixed-mount load path: measured interfaces, load sharing open

The saved **32.2 mm fixed plywood mount** now has a bounded geometric load-path check. The key result is that the existing full-circumference last-ring bond proxy overstates the nominal mount surface available at the slotted ring by **10.85%**. Even with ideal full bonding, whether thrust reaches the shell through that ring, earlier rings, the plate edge or their combination is unknown. A deliberately conservative *full thrust through the slotted shell section* sensitivity exceeds the existing estimated paper screen, so it cannot be dismissed without resolving the actual load split. It is a conditional failed sensitivity, not a demonstrated failure of the installed structure.

The next dependency is one finite, geometry-owned **plate/fingers → rails/rings → adhesive → slotted shell → forward joint** load-sharing definition, with actual bond footprints, stiffness and end restraint. That is needed to decide which shell sections carry which forces and to check ring bending and foundation stiffness. This analysis does not establish those quantities, propose another design or authorize fabrication. The primary selected this candidate for local engineering, not active adoption.

## Force paths and conditional screens

The existing G80 source applies **117.53 N forward thrust** and factor 3. The intended path is motor shoulder → three mica pads → G11 thrust plate → six bonded finger flanks and/or plate edge → plywood rails/rings → shell and forward joint. Under a symmetric equal-share hypothesis the net rail forces are 39.1767 N each, summing to 117.53 N; six equal finger forces would be 19.5883 N each. These are equilibrium bookkeeping assumptions, not solved reactions; net bending moments also cancel only under the symmetric load and reaction assumption.

If all six finite projected flank patches are fully bonded, share thrust uniformly and act in shear, their mean demand is **0.17021 MPa**, or **0.51062 MPa with DF3**. This is below the existing estimated **1 MPa hot shear screen**; the corresponding margin `1/0.51062 − 1` is approximately **+0.958**. That is a conditional uniform-shear comparison, not a real bond pass: finite stiffness, actual patch coverage, peel, edges, substrate condition and unequal load sharing are unknown.

If instead the entire force is transferred through the last-ring mount surface alone, uniform shear at **541.1142 mm²** gives **0.2172 MPa**, or **0.6516 MPa with DF3**, versus the same 1 MPa estimated hot screen, conditional margin **+0.535**. This changes the former full-circle proxy margin +0.721 and still assumes full usable wetting and no peel; it does not calculate the true patch-wise transfer or the shell-side capacity.

A separate all-force section sensitivity uses the actual **91.9144 mm²** shell cut: 117.53 N divided by that area is **1.2787 MPa** average axial demand, or **3.8361 MPa with DF3** against the source's estimated **2 MPa** kraft screen. Under that stated assumption the screen fails; a uniform axial demand no greater than **61.28 N** would satisfy that simple comparison. The 117.53 N need not flow through this particular aft section: earlier load transfer, plate edge, acceleration/inertia and forward-joint reactions can change it. That force distribution, local stress, shell tear/bond peel and ring bending remain unavailable. The estimated paper value is not a measured allowable.

For scale only, taking the existing epoxy **E=2 GPa, ν=0.3** as isotropic gives **G≈0.769 GPa**. Ideal uniform full-area shear across the 0.2 mm six-flank gaps yields **2.656×10⁶ N/mm** stiffness and about **0.0000443 mm** relative translation at 117.53 N; a similarly ideal single last-ring bond at 0.3 mm gives **1.387×10⁶ N/mm** and **0.0000847 mm**. These are rigid-adherend, perfectly filled nominal sensitivities—not installed stiffness, a validated hot modulus, a stiffness lower bound or a total assembly deflection. Ring/rail bending and shear lag, compliant shell and forward joint, adhesive coverage, thermal effects and load redistribution are excluded.

The existing candidate screen's net rail section **45.2365 mm²**, weak-axis inertia **187.441 mm⁴**, estimated hot axial and Euler margins **+0.9245 / +0.9433**, plate strip margin **+0.5036**, and reverse case **150 N / 50 N per fastener** remain conditional. The existing M3 tensile demand is **9.94 MPa** before DF3; the actual case closure, screw grade, nut capture, bearing, preload, hole edge and joint restraint are not qualified. Mica is compression-only and cannot serve as reverse retention. The modeled plate strip is not an analysis of the complete slotted ring.

## Validation and limits

Before actual BRep measurement, planar full/half overlaps returned **100/50 mm²**, and a **0.1 mm physical gap returned zero common area**. The one symmetric finger patch and two ring cylinder probes matched independent analytic dimensions to **1e-6 mm²**; the saved section and source hashes are in the record. No fuzzy gap-closing or assumed physical bond was used. The child took **5.550 s wall / 4.549 CPU s**, peak RSS **438,370,304 bytes**; OCCT pool size one and one worker were requested, without an OS-affinity claim. There was no solver, thermal or flight run and no retry.

This check supports a concrete next load-sharing model; it does not establish actual reaction distribution, installed capacity, ring bending, shell strength, stiffness, thermal fit, recovery, flutter, complete WO-03 acceptance or flight readiness. The full separate 50 g growth allowance and the existing candidate's 0–4.620 g incremental annular budget remain unchanged. G54 and G12 evidence is not transferred to this G80 package.
