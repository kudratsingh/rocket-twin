# G80 2 mm bridge: reverse-load structural preflight

**No hand check here forces a redesign, but no check establishes that the bridge can carry the reverse load.** The most useful next analysis is one bounded actual-bridge/finite-tab-adhesive case with an explicitly idealized rigid rail backing, using both equal-bolt and single-bolt loading on each of three meshes. This is a proposed numerical sensitivity, not authorization to execute FEA or a complete installed-joint model. Unknown G11 directional and hot properties, actual adhesion, rail/shell restraint, washer contact and keeper participation prevent a strength or stiffness pass.

The exact as-run source is [`bridge.step`](../build/wo03-g80-keeper-service-v1/bridge.step), SHA-256 `f92832b8cf272ef0f4a2f38b6e62312321b80f899e7efba98a004879806408ed`, and the companion [`keeper.step`](../build/wo03-g80-keeper-service-v1/keeper.step), SHA-256 `436b444410db60b87f050cb1083032c5a484ae2bdf8b1c0ed3fcad42467f91c8`, both tied to the saved [keeper/service record](../build/wo03-g80-keeper-service-v1/record.json) and integrated source identity `62e8de9eae802300bd36e5bdfbd08c8d20635d138972bb07708f574103ed7009`. The service record reports three direct washer-to-bridge nominal areas **54.5380 mm² each**, a nominal 15° nut-rotation barrier, and sampled pre-shell and cap/motor service, not qualified hardware or continuous motion. The source bridge is distinct from the earlier replan bridge: use this exact STEP, including its later M2 holes.

## Geometry and hand bounds

The G11 bridge web spans local **z=170.1–172.1 mm**, radii **15.8–32.2 mm**, with three radial rail notches beginning at **R24.9 mm**. Its six tabs are the source's 7 × 2 × 15 mm blanks, clipped by R32.2 and at three pairs of rails; the nominal rail-flank gap is **0.1 mm**. M3 washer centers at R25.5 and 60°/180°/300° lie between rail axes at 0°/120°/240°. At the R25.5 reference circle, adjacent rail axes are separated by an arc of **53.407 mm** and an intervening washer is 26.704 mm of arc from either axis. That is geometry, not a straight-beam support span or proof of where a reaction acts. At each rail-axis notch, the uncut web band from R15.8 to R24.9 is only **9.1 mm radially × 2 mm axially** before holes, local load introduction and material tolerances. It is not a complete plate section or a verified restraint.

The existing reverse service case is **150 N total**, with **DF3** applied once to demands. Equal sharing gives **50 N per washer** and nominal pressure 50/54.538 = **0.917 MPa**, or **2.750 MPa factored**. A conservative single-bolt sensitivity puts the whole 150 N on one washer: **2.750 MPa**, or **8.251 MPa factored**. These are mean projected demands, not local G11 contact/bearing stresses. The nominal one-bolt load also creates a **3.825 N·m service moment** about the bridge center (11.475 N·m after DF3); equal threefold loading cancels its in-plane moments only under exact symmetry. Screw torque, preload, friction, loss of contact and possible additional motor inertia/recovery demand are not represented by the 150 N case.

The six 15 mm tab faces have at most approximately **595.52 mm²** projected paired area under the nominal source geometry; actual overlap, glue coverage and each face's ownership were not established by the keeper/service packet. If all 150 N is transferred in axial shear over that entire area, the **minimum possible average** is **0.252 MPa**, or **0.756 MPa with DF3**, compared with the existing **1 MPa estimated hot shear screen**. It is not a pass: nonuniform load, unequal sharing, nominal gap, edge effects, peel and actual area can make the peak higher. An illustrative extreme that transfers all force through only two equally sharing full tab faces gives **2.267 MPa factored**, above that estimate, but is not a solved distribution or demonstrated actual failure. A single-bolt load does not justify assigning force to a particular number of tabs without a reaction model.

The source engineering file specifies estimated G11 **E=10 GPa**, **50 MPa cold / 25 MPa hot** screening strengths and a 150°C component sensitivity, not laminate-direction, interlaminar shear, bearing, pull-through or hot design allowables. The chosen proposed isotropic **ν=0.3** is an explicit numerical idealization; actual in-plane/out-of-plane stiffness, grain/laminate orientation and hot degradation are unmeasured. The existing adhesive study includes E=100–2000 MPa as a broad elastic sensitivity and 1 MPa hot shear as a provisional estimate; neither supplies the actual G11-to-plywood hot mixed-mode bond envelope or an admissible peel capacity. No von Mises number can by itself qualify this anisotropic laminate.

## Keeper and boundary reality

A bridge-only result does not establish zero real keeper reaction. The saved assembly has a **0.1 mm nominal broad-face gap**, but its three **integral 0.1 mm keeper pads reach the bridge** and the M2 closure screws can impose preload and transmit forces. Real plate deformation, gap tolerance, keeper movement, M2 clamp/contact and load reversal can cause additional broad-face or pad contact. The keeper is deliberately **not credited as stiffness** in the proposed single sensitivity; it has not been shown to be physically disengaged. Compare the normal motion at corresponding bridge and keeper locations against the measured/tolerance-bounded gap only after the keeper motion and preload are characterized. Even a bridge displacement below 0.1 mm relative to an assumed ground does not prove no contact, and displacement above it flags a coupled-contact concern rather than applying an invented allowable-deflection gate.

The actual plywood rails, their ring joints, paper shell and forward constraints are compliant and their installed stiffness is unknown. Rigid backing at the six external adhesive faces is an explicitly optimistic idealization for that interface; it is not a proof of a lower bound on every local stress or of no keeper loading. Treat the output as a candidate diagnostic. A passing result would leave the installed joint open; a robust, mesh-converged failure under the stated favorable assumptions is a reason to reassess this exact design, not automatically a measured failure.

## One proposed bounded case, not yet run

The full proposed parameters and stop conditions are in [the preflight configuration](../configs/studies/wo03-g80-bridge-structural-preflight.yaml). Before any solve the primary must explicitly authorize the packet and a minimal, source-pinned input generator. Existing Gmsh **4.15.2** and `/opt/homebrew/bin/ccx` **2.23** provide the intended tools; no installation, version change or production-solver edit is proposed. The existing support/finite-bond helpers are useful building blocks but their current load and boundary emitters are not this case; actual face ownership, mesh tagging, traction and report input generation need a small, reviewable adaptation, not a claim that an existing driver already implements it.

1. Import the exact saved bridge STEP, preserve all 2 mm web and M2/M3 holes, and independently identify the three measured washer patches and six finite rail-flank surfaces. Construct six **0.1 mm physical adhesive layers** only at verified paired G11/rail projection; preserve actual clipped end/curvature and no gap-closing. Reject ambiguous surfaces, overlaps, unsupported or zero interface coverage. Keep each G11/epoxy material region explicit. No actual measured wetting is asserted.
2. One initial material hypothesis uses isotropic bridge **E=10,000 MPa, ν=0.3**, epoxy **E=100 MPa, ν=0.3**, with the estimated hot 25 MPa G11 and 1 MPa epoxy shear screens reported only as comparisons, not allowables. Tie epoxy to bridge over matching finite faces, and restrain the outer epoxy faces in all translations as the declared rigid-rail approximation. These bilateral ties cannot represent debond, contact opening or realistic backing compliance; any stress/peel conclusion must carry that limitation. Exclude the keeper in the numerical model as a deliberate sensitivity, not a physical no-contact assertion.
3. Apply finite distributed normal tractions in the aft (+local z) direction on the **measured washer footprints**, with two service-load steps on the same mesh: **50/50/50 N** and **150/0/0 N** at the three source axes. Integrate forces and source-coordinate moments exactly enough to reproduce **150 N** in both steps, zero in-plane moment for the symmetric case, and **3.825 N·m** magnitude for the single-bolt case. No point load, full-circumference clamp or artificial cyclic symmetry for the asymmetric step. Apply DF3 to output demands once, not to both the applied load and the comparison capacity.
4. Before actual geometry, run the existing quadratic-tet axial known case (displacement error <1%, stress error <2%, force residual <1e-5 N) and a finite **0.1 mm** bonded shear-layer known case with analytic `u=F t/(G A)`, checking reaction, displacement and strain energy. The thin-layer test must use the same material pairing, element type, surface load transfer and intended meshing treatment; an axial-only bar does not verify shear or interface ownership. Any fixture or parser failure stops the packet.
5. Use three predefined C3D10 refinements with **1.5 / 1.0 / 0.7 mm in-plane target**, explicitly representing the actual 0.1 mm layer and 2 mm web without changing dimensions. Before solving, require valid element/node ownership and physical materials, positive sampled Jacobians, mesh-volume/area agreement, preserved gap thickness, sufficient through-thickness element order/integration, and no duplicate/overlapping bond faces. Record nodes/elements, quality and estimated memory; stop at **150,000 nodes**, **200,000 tetrahedra**, **2 GiB per-process RSS**, any invalidity or inability to enforce the guard. These are proposed resource ceilings, not assertions of feasibility. One CPU/thread requested, **≤120 s per child and ≤600 CPU s total**, including fixtures and mesh/solve; no retry or automatic cap increase.
6. Record vector force and moment balance at the six constrained backs, washer and bolt-hole displacements, strain energy, and stresses in fixed physical regions: bridge web around each washer and M2/M3 bore, tab roots/fillets, adhesive mid-faces/edges and nominal minimum web near each notch. Report full component tensors and signed normal/peel and shear, not only von Mises. For meaningful non-singular regional averages, require the existing **5% refinement comparison** without widening it; report the literal three values and all singular edge peaks, which are not converted to a pass if they fail to converge. Check reaction force residual at the existing **1e-5 N** fixture scale and report moment residual with units; material limits remain unqualified. Record no-contact/clearance as **unavailable** without keeper-relative displacement and preload.

The actual interface footprint, mesh-generation feasibility, stress concentrations, coupled keeper contact and installed backing are unresolved. No mesh, FEA, thermal solve, new vehicle or physical test was run in this preflight. The primary retains the analysis authorization and engineering decision.
