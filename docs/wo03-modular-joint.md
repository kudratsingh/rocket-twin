## Actual construction and load path

The common upper retains its paper coupler at 868–972 mm, Ri 30.5 / Ro 32 mm.
An explicit 6.079 g cured-epoxy annulus fills the actual 0.5 mm radial upper-shell
gap over 868–918 mm; this mass is additional to the finish allowance. Two G11
bearing lands, Ri 32.1 / Ro 32.35 mm, occupy 924–934 and 962–972 mm, with modeled
0.1 mm radial bond films. Their intended separated contact reactions transfer
fin bending into the coupler. Paper compression, contact arc, ring flexibility,
ovalisation, coupler bending section and peel are not validated by that intent.

The printed stop retains four pieces. Its keys now span radii 30.6–33.6 mm over
922–947 mm and physically cross the 32.5–33 mm aft-shell wall. Actual axial
cylindrical machining cuts create open-ended passages: 6.6 mm for the 5.2 mm key,
4.8 mm for each 3.2 mm key. One wide key rejects the 120° and 240° wrong indices.
Key bonding remains a process dependency; no structural torque or axial-retention
capacity is credited to the indexing pieces.

Three radial M3×10 screw hypotheses at 30°, 150° and 270°, station 946 mm, pass
through actual 3.4 mm bores in the shell, coupler, curved G11 pads and bond films.
The 32 mm-long inner pads are Ri 28.3 / Ro 30.3 mm; the outer saddles are
Ri 33.2 / Ro 34.5 mm. Normal `tube` and `machining_holes` generators produce their
three arc contours. Screw/head, washer and nut purchased envelopes have a combined
6 g estimated mass, replacing the old generic 6 g box. A separate 0.9 g budget
counts unresolved nut capture and key adhesive once.

There is **no positive captured-nut geometry**. A radial cylinder does not establish
hex flats, antirotation or a captive lip. The YAML records the needed rotated
5.7±0.1 mm across-flats pocket, 7 mm axial length, 2.6 mm radial depth and positive
cap/lip; the normal CAD contract needs an explicit supported pocket/capture feature.
Exact screw/nut/washer maker, grade, locking, torque, drive access and mass remain
dependencies. The modeled nut envelope leaves a 25.9 mm minimum bore radius.

No bypass harness is modeled. The conditional common-upper-only assignment is

At the separately specified **180 N already-factored front-joint force**, each
screw carries 60 N. The declared hand screens yield 91.468 MPa combined bolt

screen does not establish paper load spreading, stop end-crush, complete net

For G54 full recovery, combined bolt stress is **949.240 MPa** and outer-pad
bearing is **159.659 MPa**; margins are −0.684 and −0.843. The upper-only case
still produces 411.529 MPa bolt stress and 69.218 MPa outer bearing. Bond average

The active 25 kPa / 5° fin input gives 31.129 N per fin. Conservatively aligning
all three panel forces at their aftmost station gives a DF3 joint moment of
77.886 N·m. The proposed 38 mm bearing separation requires a 2,189.711 N peak
reaction. Even optimistic full projected paper contact is **3.384 MPa versus the
2 MPa screen**; quarter projected contact is 13.538 MPa. The paper bearing path
therefore fails this conservative screen before a complete coupler bending/peel
analysis. The radial bolts alone would need 1,682.623 N at the most loaded bolt;
they cannot silently substitute for the failed bearing path.

## Resume conditions and evidence limits

Resume with one integrated structural correction: close recovery load allocation
with actual harness geometry, strengthen the fin/coupler load path, implement
positive captive nuts, and correct the minimum fits while retaining unique key
rejection. Reconcile the resulting complete mass and G12 guide margin; the present
17.230 g increment already fails the finned trainer departure screen. Do not
adopt a fixed-mode collar without its actual common-upper connection and mass.

Strength bounds are estimates, not laminate or fastener allowables. Equations use
basic equilibrium and bearing/shear/bending relations described in
[NASA RP-1228](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19900009424). The
[ISO 3506-1 scope](https://www.iso.org/standard/70045.html) explicitly excludes
functional shear strength and torque/clamp behavior; it does not validate the
estimated 300/150 MPa bolt bounds. The
[ASA manufacturer sheet](https://prusament.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/ASA_Prusament_TDS_2022_16_EN.pdf)
reports test-specific properties and warns that process and conditions matter.
Adherend-specific wet/hot coupons, capture/service checks, joint bending/pull
proof and weighed hardware remain later owner-performed verification. No FEA,
purchase, physical work, flight release or active-geometry adoption is claimed.
