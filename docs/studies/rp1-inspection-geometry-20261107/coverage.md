# Frozen AD1 inspection geometry coverage

All 55 frozen source part records retain their exact stable IDs. Source-dimensioned
solids show estimated nominal design intent. Simplified envelopes are visual
reservations. Mass allowances have no solid because a scalar budget is not a
spatial allocation. Missing geometry names the specific missing installed shape.
None of these categories establishes a manufactured fit, physical mass/inertia,
engineering adoption, browser interaction or flight acceptance.
