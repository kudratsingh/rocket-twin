## 1. Sidebars that open and close from the same place

The default desktop capture shows an unlabeled menu icon at the far right of the header, two header buttons that duplicate the Parts and Details tabs, and a separate × inside the sidebar. The existing evidence covers all four combinations of open and closed panels at 1280 × 720. It also records a working narrow drawer after a correction. The owner still needs a control that makes both directions apparent. [Default Chrome capture](../frontend-release-20261107/browser-evidence-pack/artifacts/rp1-side-tools-closed-default-guide-fixed-1440x900.png) · [Header actions](../../web/src/workspaces/WorkspaceChrome.tsx#L161) · [Sidebar actions](../../web/src/workspaces/InspectionWorkspace.tsx#L267) · [RV-Q09 browser evidence](../../docs/studies/frontend-release-20261107/browser-results.csv#L6)

- Place independent **Show navigation / Hide navigation** and **Show parts sidebar / Hide parts sidebar** controls at a stable left edge or in a small strip beside the viewport. Keep their labels or clear icons with visible text where space permits. Both controls stay reachable when their panels are closed. Use Parts and Details as tabs within the sidebar.
- Keep the selected design, part, Parts or Details tab, sidebar width and camera angle through close and reopen. Return keyboard focus to the corresponding toggle when closing from within a panel. Escape closes the narrow drawer and returns focus to its opener. Focus mode keeps its existing restoration behavior and a clear exit.
- Use the same control for pointer and keyboard activation, with an accessible name and accurate expanded state. Preserve the existing desktop canvas targets of at least 72% viewport width and 78% height with tools closed. Retest after toggling and resizing, including at 900 × 720 where the context sidebar becomes a drawer. [Layout criteria](../../docs/frontend-revamp-acceptance.csv)

## 2. Camera control from the viewport

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom and right-drag to pan already exist. Their instructions are at the bottom of the Scene tools drawer. At 1440 × 900, opening that drawer reduced the captured canvas height from 782 to 536 CSS pixels. The Camera menu provides presets and fit/reset, and the axis widget is currently a passive indicator. The prior Chrome run covers one manual rotation and a resize followed by explicit Fit. The full rotation range and keyboard operation still need an interaction check. [Layout measurements](../../docs/studies/frontend-release-20261107/layout-capture-measurements.md) · [Orbit controls](../../web/src/components/Viewer.tsx#L375) · [Camera menu](../../web/src/components/Viewer.tsx#L602) · [Axis widget](../../web/src/components/Viewer.tsx#L728) · [Gesture hint](../../web/src/components/Viewer.tsx#L919) · [Saved camera check](../../docs/studies/frontend-release-20261107/browser-results.csv#L11)

- Put **Orbit / camera** and a compact gesture hint beside the 3D viewport, reachable with Scene tools closed. Give the user continuous 360° horizontal rotation, tilt from nose to motor end, and roll about the viewing axis. Expose the actual angles in degrees through labeled keyboard-operable controls. Use consistent vehicle axes for the angle readout and define stable behavior looking along either end. Include mouse and trackpad gestures plus equivalent controls that do not require dragging.
- Retain the familiar presets as shortcuts. Label a manually adjusted view **Custom**, and update the readout after a drag, keyboard change or preset. Distinguish **Fit assembly**, **Frame selection** and **Reset camera**. Fit and Frame retain the chosen angle, while Reset returns to Overview. Give the orbit an explicit target of the assembly or selected group/part so a tiny component can be turned without swinging around the entire rocket.
- Keep the custom pose, target and selection when changing panels or resizing. Check for unwanted clipping during orbit, close zoom and pullback on both the assembly and a small part. The source sets near/far clipping planes during fit, so the test should cover manual motion after fitting too. Avoid intercepting keys while the user types in search or angle inputs. ## 3. A 3D breakdown that reveals components

The current model has **10 source-dimensioned shapes and 16 simplified envelopes**. The remaining records are **25 mass or process allowances and four missing geometries**. The saved isolated barometer shows that a single mesh can be framed cleanly. It also shows why the user needs per-part labels for simplified geometry and a way to see the part in its subsystem. The current tree and visibility controls operate on individual IDs, and the source relationship labels “Attached to” and “Supports” can imply a physical interface from a parent field alone. Use verified connection evidence for those statements. [Isolated barometer capture](../frontend-release-20261107/browser-evidence-pack/artifacts/rp1-baro-isolated-unannotated-tools-closed-guide-fixed-1440x900.png) · [RP1 geometry coverage](../../docs/studies/rp1-inspection-geometry-20261107/coverage.md) · [Current tree](../../web/src/components/PartsTree.tsx#L23) · [Current relationship labels](../../web/src/components/InspectionPanel.tsx#L245)

The current 100% explode calculation leaves **115.97 mm** of overlapping axial bounds between the coaxial motor and motor tube. The parachute’s whole axial interval also stays inside the upper body’s interval. All 55 RP1 offsets in the source are zero, so the renderer uses a fallback based on each mesh’s center. These are bounds from the current display transform, and explain why sliding to full explode can fail to reveal nested parts. The saved browser record leaves the explode branch unexecuted. [Explode transform](../../web/src/components/Viewer.tsx#L123) · [Recorded mesh bounds](../../docs/studies/rp1-inspection-geometry-20261107/manifest.json) · [RV-Q17 browser evidence](../../docs/studies/frontend-release-20261107/browser-results.csv#L13)

- Add a presentation tree for **Nose and airframe**, **Fins and roots**, **Motor and retention**, **Recovery**, **Avionics and power**, and **Launch guides and finishing**. Map it to the exact RP1 source and existing part IDs. Show group counts and each row’s status as source-dimensioned, simplified, mass allowance or missing geometry. Keep one primary membership for each source record and allow cross-links for shared interfaces. Expose the original parent field as source provenance. Give a bundled set one visible entry with its quantity where recorded, and keep integral fin panels and tabs together during display separation.
- Put **Assembled**, **Breakdown** and **Section** beside the viewport. Give groups and individual parts visible **Frame** and **Isolate** actions where geometry exists. In Breakdown, separate assemblies first, then let the user open a group and separate its supported parts. Move the motor away from its tube and reveal the recovery contents from the upper body. Use the same display transforms for drawing, picking and fitting. Zero separation returns meshes to their source placement. **Exit isolate** restores the prior visibility, and **Restore assembly** returns to the assembled view with the selection retained.
- Synchronize selected and highlighted parts between the model, Parts and Details. Expand the selected part’s group and reveal its row on a model pick. Show the visible mesh count separately from the record count. For a meshless selection such as `rp1_kevlar_wrap`, keep Details and the recorded drawing available and make the unavailable Frame and Isolate actions explicit. Check both the Scene tools and Details action paths. The actual store currently hides all 26 mesh-bearing records when asked to isolate the wrap. ## 4. Compare alternatives from the selected component

Start with the fin/root assembly. Add **Compare alternatives** to its group and part details. Open current AD1 and recorded FP1/FR1 studies together with their source identity, dimensions or mass basis where recorded, and the actual failed, estimated or unknown findings. Both studies are unadopted. Their current assets are separate schematic relationship diagrams, so give each an explicit 3D availability label and a link to its drawing. The general configuration picker remains available for separately identified archived vehicles. [RP1 study comparison](../../docs/wo04-rp1-studies-20261104.md) · [Current schematic view](../../web/src/workbench/Rp1StudyComparison.tsx#L275) · [Candidate sidebar](../../web/src/components/PartsTree.tsx#L144)

Where exact source dimensions and placement support a recorded alternative and the work order admits an inspection representation, show its own labeled 3D preview with its source and geometry identity. Use a shared scale and orientation for comparable geometry. Where evidence is incomplete, show the missing dimensions or interfaces alongside the schematic. Only compute differences for compatible recorded cases. Closing comparison returns to the original RP1 group, part, camera and visibility. Treat comparison as inspection, with the active design and simulation source explicitly pinned. [Inspection source rules](../../docs/adr/0016-rp1-frontend-revamp.md)
