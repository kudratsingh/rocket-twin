**Research snapshot, 2026-11-04. No equipment is known to be owned, ordered, calibrated for this method, or used.** This packet supports the prepared [D1 test definition](wo03-rp1-d1-test-definition.md) and [load/attachment decision](wo03-rp1-load-attachment-decision.md). The matching machine-readable source register is [`rp1-vp1-equipment-sources.yaml`](../configs/studies/rp1-vp1-equipment-sources.yaml).

## Route and scope

1. **Documentation first.** Request actual stock, layup, adhesive-process, conditioned and hot joint data using the prepared unsent material questions. If these bound the finite-root question, do not make a home fixture.
2. **Small coupon stage if documents do not suffice.** A later owner-reviewed protocol can use controlled masses and a direct, support-referenced indicator on a bare-ply bending coupon. The primary's revised planning specimen is 270 × 25 mm, 240 mm primary and 180 mm held-out spans, and at most about 10 N / 4 MPa for *effective flexural compliance only*. Those dimensions and load limits are planning scope, not an executed test or a shear-G/strength measurement. The earlier D1 150 × 25 mm, 60/120/90 mm geometry is a different method-development proposal; do not silently mix records. With 0.01 mm graduation and ±0.013 mm indicator accuracy, the short-span two-span G inversion is unlikely to be identifiable without a demonstrated uncertainty budget. The gauge contact force (up to 1.4 N) is material next to a 10 N coupon load and must be included in force balance and zero/reversal checks. A nominal 1 kg mass is only a candidate reference; the actual certified mass, hanger mass, local gravity, support friction, load point and total uncertainty need to be recorded. No deadweight load or compliance is claimed exact.
3. **Full root stage by supervised service quote.** D1's 240 mm local installed article requires **331.125 N and 8.83 N·m, already factored once**, independent body/root, tube/ring and tip motions, and a force/moment uncertainty budget. Two generalized force inputs of 147.166667 N at 10 mm and 183.958333 N at 40 mm match the root force/moment pair but do not alone reproduce distributed pressure or full-fin displacement. The presently priced stand and gauge have one 500 N channel, with no qualified two-input fixture or moment calibration. A written method and price from a supervised mechanical/composites laboratory is the bounded next equipment decision. Service cost, specimen preparation, and fixture design remain **unknown**.

## Reusable, consumed and unpriced scope

**Reusable, priced candidates:** measuring instruments, bases, general mass balance and check mass above. A one-point coupon setup is cheaper than outfitting the complete root, but even the affordable indicator needs an independent support reference and its ±0.013 mm accuracy/contact force in the uncertainty budget. For a four-location D1 root measurement, indicator count and placement must follow the approved method, not a shopping list default.

**Consumed or built scope, unpriced:** exact selected stock and adhesive, witness strips, 240 mm body and motor tube, fasteners, a 60/120/90 or revised 240/180 mm bending support set as applicable, safe mass hanger, steel metrology plate, loading noses, calibrated span jig, guards, moisture conditioning enclosure/supplies, and finishes. Their source/geometry is not yet selected. A guarded screw or lever fixture is physically imaginable for coupons but fixture friction, alignment, reaction compliance, pinched contact, and failure containment need an owner-reviewed design. The Mark-10 stand is not a substitute for that design. No live motor is part of any test article.

**Professional service, unpriced:** [Westmoreland Mechanical Testing & Research](https://wmtr.com/en.composites.html) states custom composite testing, customer-specific fixtures, conditioning, and an on-site machine shop; its published contact number is **724-537-3131**. Its ability to execute this particular mixed force/moment root method, instrument uncertainty, schedule, specimens and price is **unconfirmed until a written quote**. No contact was made. A quote request should ask separately for (a) actual-lot flexural coupons and selected bonded/termination witnesses with conditioning; (b) a 240 mm body/tube/ring root article, 331.125 N plus 8.83 N·m, reverse and pure/combined cases, independent body/root/motor/ring/tip motion and failure observations; (c) their proposed distributed-pressure equivalence and uncertainty method; (d) specimen fabrication, fixture design, calibration traceability, hot/wet conditioning, deliverables, lead time and itemized cost. Do not imply ASTM conformity for the custom geometry or acceptance from a generic lab capability page.

## Decision and stop conditions

The economical next action is supplier/process documentation. If that does not close the material uncertainty, the owner can review a single low-load coupon protocol with a measured force and displacement uncertainty budget. Stop the low-load interpretation if fixture motion, indicator contact force, specimen variation or calibration dominates the signal; it cannot establish shear G, hot finished strength, joint peel or installed root stiffness. A home 500 N gauge plus ES20 purchase would leave the decisive fixture and multichannel problem open. Obtain a written supervised-service method and quote before choosing the high-load route. The present packet authorizes no purchase, contact, fabrication, loading, change in D1 acceptance, or adoption of FP1/FR1.
