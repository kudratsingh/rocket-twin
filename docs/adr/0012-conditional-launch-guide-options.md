# ADR-0012 — Conditional launch-guide options in WO-03

Status: accepted for study/planning; no launch-baseline adoption or flight approval.
Date: 2026-11-01 local /2026-11-02 UTC.

The owner authorized a bounded Sol HIGH motor/guide comparison in parallel
with one connected G80 aft receiver. The source1.5m usable guide, formal15m/s,
separate15.5m/s planning target and full separate50g remain unchanged. The
previous198.333g planning /252.877g formal replacement budgets are conditional
on that source motor, guide, friction/drag/thrust and retained-mass model.
Approximately1kg is not a general rocket limit. The physical project ceiling
remains strictly below1500g including growth and is not a target mass.

For planning, prefer G80 with a **confirmed supported compatible1.8m usable
guide** if it can be provided at the actual club. This gives source-model
replacement budgets357.990g at15.5 and421.054g at15, with full growth once.
Fallback: a lighter G80 arrangement satisfying the unchanged1.5m baseline.
An independently confirmed2.4m usable supported rail is a further option,
not established local access. G54 remains conditional with its own source,
hardware, certification/state and integration gates. G33's saved hypothesis
fails this package's guide screen over1.5–3m; G12 remains the controls track.

The saved guide CAD has rod lugs, not1010/1515 rail buttons. Actual overall
rail length must be reduced by the rocket's initial upper-guide height and
release/end/stop geometry to obtain safe two-guide travel. No single-guide
interval earns assumed credit. MDARS publishes approximately10ft1515 and
1010 backed by1515; ROC publishes rail types but no lengths. Neither source
confirms our event access, configuration acceptance, stiffness or wind limit.
The preferred option is therefore a dependency, not an adopted specification.

Before changing a resolved launch baseline, record club/site/date, exact rail
and support/stop dimensions, compatible button/attachment geometry and mass,
usable travel, operating limits and complete-configuration departure results.
Repeat affected structural, fit, static and flight/reference checks as needed.
Longer rails do not close strength, heat, stability, recovery or legal eligibility.

The S3 connected receiver is not admitted:1199.622g with growth and0.205cal
loaded margin; even the illustrative10ft setup gives15.146m/s against the
separate15.5 planning target. Motor-removal and recovery-cap load issues also
remain. Its dimensioned load-path work is retained without adopting the
heavy construction. No G80 result transfers to G54 or G12/PID-to-RL.

Evidence: [launch study](../wo03-launch-guide-motor-trade.md),
[connected receiver](../wo03-g80-connected-receiver.md). WO-03 remains open.
The work-order sequence, early preview exception and physical/purchase/paid/
WO-10 owner-review/flight-release gates are unchanged.
