# ADR-0013 — Dedicated conventional first G80

Status: accepted owner direction; engineering implementation and release open.
Date:2026-11-03 UTC. Supersedes only mandatory physical interchangeability for
the first conventional G80, not the G54 or G12/PID-to-RL commitments.

The owner explicitly prioritizes a simpler, custom-built fixed-motor G80.
Shared specification/schema, instrumentation, JAX physics, RocketPy validation,
frontend and learning infrastructure remain. Shared physical upper tubes,
removable afts and control hardware are optional trades, not G80 requirements.
No servo/gimbal provision is required in this conventional vehicle.

`G80-F1` is a new candidate identity, distinct from historical C1, A/B and A1.
The interrupted A2 intake contains research/inventory and unexecuted frontend
scaffolding only; no A2 design calculation was run. Preserve it without adoption.

Select one conventional construction using manufacturer manuals as precedents:
continuous commercial kraft tube, custom printed nose, custom TTW plywood fins,
fixed paper29mm motor tube and plywood centering rings, motor-mount recovery
anchor, flexible heat leader/shock harness and independent electronic recovery
plus recorded motor-ejection hypothesis. No unmodified kit substitutes for our
custom design. Exact stock, fit, supplier motor/retainer and all departures need
source traceability and appropriate calculations.

Review project recovery assumptions by physical mechanism. Preserve old
20.4m/s/.3m/DF3 records; do not treat the mean stopping equation as an inflation
peak or apply it to every section regardless of topology. Any revised design
case must state deployment timing, line stretch, participating mass, evidence,
uncertainty, operating/fault rationale and before/after consequences. No load or
material limit changes merely to obtain a pass. Legal limits remain.

Distinguish a calculated package for an owner-authorized fit/prototype build
from physically qualified hardware and flight release. Missing physical tests
are open verification, not demonstrated design failures. Critical unresolved
dimensions or unacceptable calculated loads still block the affected build.
Full expanded WO03/04/05 acceptance remains open, with G54/G12 mode evidence
separate. WO09 final procurement/qualified instructions, WO10 owner firmware
review, physical/purchase/paid/launch gates and sequence remain.
