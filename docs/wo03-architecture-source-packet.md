## At most two complete architecture directions

These are source-informed engineering inferences for the primary to freeze,
not dimensioned candidates, admitted analyses or a request for another review
gate. Retain the nonmetallic structural scope and substantial custom building.

**A — conventional motor-fin can with a closed structural tube.** Keep the
external fin planform, use sandwich panels with full dense roots passing through
the body into a real structural motor/fin tube, and connect that tube through
two broad ring interfaces into the body/upper joint. Put continuous closed-shell
torsion and accessible long root/ring/body laps into the actual construction.
Use specified positive root capture where needed, not C2's narrow glued receiver
pad. Independent aft motor retention and its removal sweep stay separate from
recovery anchors. A thermal liner or commercial motor casing is not credited
as the structural tube. Apogee establishes the topology, not capacity: the full
root/tube/ring/body load path still needs finite compliance, bond peel and hot
directional evidence. Count every tube, ring, fillet, insert, fastener, process
allowance, shielding and retained body/recovery function.

**B — captured roots in a closed load-bearing sleeve with distributed recovery.**
Keep sandwich/dense-root fins in mechanically closed sockets tied into an actual
full-circumference structural sleeve and end diaphragms. Preserve large-area
torsion transfer instead of substituting three small cells without a global
stiffness account. Route a continuous recovery backbone over broad rounded,
inspectable interfaces into explicit upper and aft component terminations,
outside the independent motor-retention sweep. Closed loops may distribute
contact, but unequal legs, seam strength, saddle bending, chafe, body transfer
and tensile cap reactions remain explicit. This combines the observed methods
with engineering judgment; no reviewed source certifies this exact arrangement.
Count the finished loops/eyes/stitches/chafe, diaphragms, positive joints,
service space and shell mass. Do not assume equal sharing or credit raw webbing
capacity through a single M3 or an unqualified paper cut.

For either direction, freeze a complete connection graph and mass/function
ledger before execution. Stop on any failed geometric, coupled-budget or hot
strength/motion screen; no automatic variants or open-ended solver work follows.
