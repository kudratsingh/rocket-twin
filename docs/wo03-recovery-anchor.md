# WO-03 direct recovery anchor: wider ports and saddles

The later [complete bill and forward-cap/opening-load audit](wo03-recovery-budget-closure.md)
supersedes this page's 14-end mass envelope for integrated decisions. It counts
18 ends, an actual junction and forward-frame material; it also executes nominal
CAD and exposes link/servo interferences and rail load-transfer failures. The
figures below remain the original isolated prototype results, not current
integrated acceptance evidence.

**Prototype only; not adopted or qualified.** This revision puts the G11 ports on a 10 mm pitch and adds integral rounded saddles. The analytical section screens pass their estimated limits for the listed cases, but the conservative illustrative splice-length budget pushes the G12 candidate above the projected mass headroom. The splice material and actual end count have not been measured; neither the 200 mm-per-end budget nor the earlier 90 mm return is demonstrated to comply with the manufacturer procedure. G11 bond peel, anchoring, thermal protection, exact routing, full assembly and owner-performed tests remain open.

## Rounded anchor geometry

The main line branches at station 0.60 m to three aft bridle legs; nose, body and electronics have separately attached lines. Paired ports are at `(22, ±5) mm` fixed or `(28, ±5) mm` TVC, rotated 0°, 120° and 240°. Each pair has an integral 6 mm-long radial G11 cylinder of radius 3 mm, centered on the aft web face; the nominal exposed half-cylinder supplies a 6 mm diameter contact profile. Three saddles are **Boolean-fused into each actual part**, not added as standalone mass estimates. The minimum assumed hole pitch is 9.8 mm, and maximum port diameters are 4.0 mm fixed and 3.8 mm TVC. The resulting minimum flat ligament is 5.8/6.0 mm: at least twice an **assumed 2.9 mm maximum cord diameter**, versus 2.5 mm nominal. Actual rope tolerance, asymmetric port placement, saddle fillets, cord path and contact must be measured. This relation does not prove the full saddle eye, splice throat or physical compatibility with [Samson's 2:1 bearing and 3:1 eye-length guidance](https://www.samsonrope.com/warning-statement).

Fixed anchor: 9.5 mm nominal / 9.2 mm minimum G11 web, six Ø3.9 ±0.1 mm ports and the existing plywood frame. Shift the fore plate forward 0.5 mm to preserve the motor-front/pad reference; installed fit must be checked. G54 uses 42 mm fingers, ending at 1.0425 m after the shift versus the proposed brace at 1.045 m (2.5 mm gap). G80's frame has a nearer ring at 1.100 m: use **35 mm** fingers, ending at 1.0945 m after the same shift (5.5 mm gap), rather than extending 42 mm into it. The G80 35 mm area is used in its bond screen. Temporary CAD Boolean results are valid single solids: G80 **+3.805 g**, G54 **+4.764 g** at 1,850 kg/m³; the three exposed saddle portions account for approximately 0.486 g of each. These volume differences include the thicker web, real clipped fingers, saddles and drilled holes; the 0.5 mm placement change is not an installed interference check. A hole's maximum nominal-center radial extent is √(22²+5²)+2 = 24.56 mm, inside the 25 mm notch-start radius; tolerance on the port center itself and local saddle/contact need actual CAD verification.

TVC anchor: fixed gimbal frame, **8.0 mm nominal / 7.7 mm minimum** front thickness, front inner radius 24 mm and existing rear 30.3 mm radius, six Ø3.7 ±0.1 mm ports. The temporary Boolean uses the actual source frame, fuses the front ring and saddles and cuts six bores; the nominal one-solid volume gives **+17.469 g**, including 0.353 g exposed saddles; the 7.7 mm minimum BRep gives **+16.770 g**. G11 saddle overlap and hole cuts are counted by Boolean union, not by summing ideal annuli. The 48 mm front ID exceeds nominal 43 mm cassette OD; the 8 mm ring ends at 1.028 m and its 3 mm saddle top reaches 1.031 m, 5 mm before the cited 1.036 m stop plane. It remains to demonstrate full swept motion, front insertion, thermal spacing, port access, existing rail intersections and integrated native per-ring geometry. The tether must not attach to or restrict the moving cassette.

The out-of-plane bridge-bending estimate is **80 MPa at or below 150 °C**, only for XY laminate with bending through Z. [BASFF's G11 H-class sheet](https://www.basff.it/fileup/Immagini/G11%20ENG.pdf) reports typical perpendicular-to-lamination ISO 178 flexural strength of 450 MPa at 20 °C and 350 MPa after 1 h at 150 °C. The 80 MPa design estimate is below one-quarter of the hot typical value; loads already include factor three. This is not a material minimum, measured lot allowable or other-mode screen. Traceable BASFF grade or qualified equivalent, orientation and representative hot coupons are required. Bearing and shear retain the project's **25 MPa** estimated hot screen, adhesive retains **1 MPa**, and Atlas data are not used. The earlier 25 MPa bridge comparison failed its then-generic screen; it is historical, not retroactively a pass. See [G11 load properties](wo03-g11-load-properties.md).

## Rope, splices, temperature and mass

[Samson AmSteel-Blue 7/64 in](https://www.samsonrope.com/mooring/amsteel--blue) is listed as nominal 2.5 mm **eight-strand**, 4.5 g/m and minimum spliced strength 1,400 lbf (6,228 N). Average strength is not used. Its [8-strand Class II eye-splice instructions](https://www.samsonrope.com/docs/default-source/splice-instructions/8strand_c2_eye-splice_jul2012_web.pdf) specify **16 picks** of rope preparation, six complete tucks, three further tucks, taper and three final tucks. They do not specify an equivalent 90 mm or fixed millimeter allowance for this particular product. Pick spacing, eye size, ends and workmanship must be measured and the selected termination verified; 90 mm is not established as compliant. The figure's 8–10 inch cut-strand directions must not be misread as a specified 8–10 inch full-rope bury.

The explicit **provisional cutting envelope** counts 1.26 m for three 0.42 m standing legs, 1.5 m for upper/nose/sled branch paths, **14 end terminations × 0.20 m = 2.8 m**, and 1.2 m main line. Fourteen is a conservative layout assumption: two ends each for main, three aft legs and three other branches. It must be replaced by a full physical line diagram, manufacturer-required pick measurements and as-built end count. Total is **6.76 m**, with 0.25 m of the main line replaced by an estimated aramid hot-end leader. At 4.5 g/m HMPE and estimated 8 g/m leader, line mass is **31.30 g**. The 200 mm blank per end is an engineering envelope, not a supplier guarantee, and excludes any unmodeled junction complexity. A verified pre-made termination or different continuous-loop arrangement could change the count, but no lighter design is credited before its construction and strength are shown.

Samson lists generic HMPE critical temperature of **65.5 °C** and application-dependent creep in its [technical warning manual](https://www.samsonrope.com/docs/default-source/default-document-library/warning-insert.pdf). The counted 3 g sleeve and 0.25 m leader do not prove protection from ejection gas, localized heating or abrasion. Motor-frame temperature histories are not tether temperatures. The line path through the sealed upper bulkhead must clear avionics, switch, gimbal and hot case, permit aft separation and repacking, maintain pressure sealing and avoid pyro exposure; demonstrate these with actual hardware and owner tests. If HMPE thermal or splice closure fails, a suitable high-temperature line is an alternative only after its terminations and actual installed mass are established; the former 400 lbf aramid cord failed its 50%-strength one-leg sensitivity.

The mass book includes 2 g terminal work, 1.174 g body bands, 2.165 g sled tabs, 1.5 g port/seal, 3 g sleeve, 2 g adhesive and 0.8 g provisional positive-pin increment, subtracting the existing 10 g cord and 4 g eye. The original 5 mm recovery bulkhead stays; no unimplemented plate saving is credited. The selected thin carrier contributes **+4.765 g**, replacing the previous +6.918 g carrier correction; it is an estimate from the separate packaging proposal and must not be counted twice.

Conversely, the 3 g sleeve and 2 g terminal allowances may understate real thermal or connector hardware. A quantified cut plan and measured components are required before claiming the budget passes.

## Loads and estimated screens

The 3× force screen uses 0.3 m constant stopping distance and current resolved masses, all 50 g allowance and candidate growth. The G54 12 s no-canopy velocity 23.139 m/s is an off-nominal timing probe, not a measured opening transient or approved delay; electronic apogee remains primary. If separate upper branches all engage, the total G54 main demand is **2,456 N** and the aft anchor **1,596 N**. The fixed fore plate's own inertia is subtracted before the six-finger rail transfer; the resulting mean shear is **0.939 MPa** against the 1 MPa estimate. This assumes all six 6.2 × 42 mm faces carry equal load; shear lag, local peel, temperature, moisture and assembled bond contact remain open. G80 at its 12 s probe gives **0.903 MPa** on six 6.2 × 35 mm faces. Applying the whole aft-assembly force to motor-retainer fasteners would overstate their branch; the G54 loaded motor alone has about 536 N factored inertia, and its own reverse retention still needs a real capacity check. Upper body, nose, electronics, fin roots and fixed/TVC frame interfaces need separate attachment and load closure.

For port bearing, the model uses 9.2/7.7 mm minimum plate thickness, 3.8/3.6 mm minimum port diameter and two equally loaded hole sides. For ligament shear it uses 4.0/3.8 mm maximum diameter and 9.8 mm minimum pitch. The conservative unsupported bridge beam uses 10.2 mm maximum pitch and those minimum section dimensions; the BRep saddle is **not credited** as increased section modulus. A leg takes the entire aft load in the one-leg bound. A matched estimate also considers 80–200 kN per-leg EA, 420 mm loaded length and 1 mm maximum loaded-length spread; these are unmeasured assumptions and not demonstrated for eight-strand HMPE.

Compare bearing/shear with 25 MPa and bridge bending with the mode-specific 80 MPa estimate. G54 bearing is only about 9.6% below its estimated limit. These are simple average/beam proxies with unverified equal load between the two ports, laminate and contact. The 6 mm nominal saddle diameter is not proof that a loaded cord sees that complete smooth diameter; tolerance, edge finishing and contact test evidence are necessary.

Half the 6,228 N Samson minimum is about 3,114 N, which exceeds both 2,456 N whole-system and 1,596 N G54 single-leg demands. This **50% retained-strength sensitivity** is not an installed splice or rocket-system rating and cannot establish compliance with the maker's geometry, shock, thermal or termination instructions. No manufacturer WLL is compared to already factored force.

Before acceptance, complete actual native CAD, thermal and full-motion checks; validate end count, pick length, eyes, splice, saddle, shock response and load share; resolve bond peel and independent component retention; reconcile actual carrier/pins, masses and G12 guide, static/flight and WO-03 engineering checks on the final copies. Physical fabrication, proof/deployment tests and purchases remain owner-performed stages.
