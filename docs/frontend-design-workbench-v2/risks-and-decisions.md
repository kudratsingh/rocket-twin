No new owner answer is required to start P1. Hardware is unpurchased and supplier/
lab messages must remain unsent. Do not request owner measurements of unowned
parts. Later missing evidence is presented with the smallest exact dimension,
product choice or representative test that would resolve it, after the reviewable
package exists. Purchases, contact, physical work, paid services, firmware review
and flight keep their existing authorization points.

The plan authorizes virtual composition and normal local implementation. It does
not turn the generic parts bank into a verified supply inventory, imply California/
club eligibility from a label, adopt a longer guide, revise a structural/thermal
screen, or make PID/RL optional. Engineering decisions remain with the primary and
traceable to the applicable work order.
