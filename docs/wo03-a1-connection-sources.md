## Owner measurement and proof plan after analytical admission

Planning only; physical work requires the owner's later authorization and a protected procedure.

1. **Freeze the purchased forms and inspect the fit stack.** Record exact Acculam or alternative manufacturer, tube grade/form/layup, lot, nominal and ordered tolerances; end-ring, body, coupler and motor/retainer identities. Measure tube ID/OD, wall and ovality at both ends and connection stations; hole position/diameter, edge distance, fastener/insert dimensions and surface defects. Record moisture condition and temperature. Use the measured extrema with the unchanged 0.1 mm radial-clearance requirement and motor removal sweep; do not infer clearance from nominal 29 mm motor diameter.
2. **Establish tube thermal and moisture behavior.** Obtain a supplier/lot report or condition representative stock to the declared dry, humid and hot conditions and measure axial/circumferential fit change through the relevant burn/soak profile. Treat sheet CTE and 24-hour uptake only as context. Retain the project's 45 °C substrate screen and full required thermal matrix; do not use a 180 °C temperature-index label as permission to exceed them.
3. **Make the actual load path testable.** If the bounded geometry and complete mass ledger first pass, specify coupons cut/drilled from the chosen tube with the actual tool, support, edge finish, wall thickness, fiber orientation, hole clearance and edge distance. Compare pin/bearing, net section, shear-out and delamination under representative hot/wet and dry conditions, and inspect permanent hole growth. Then test representative root/tube/ring/body segments with the actual moment arm and adjoining compliance, monitoring shear, peel, slip and reversible movement. Agree load levels and acceptance criteria for the admitted configuration before any test; a small coupon does not prove the installed joint.
4. **Prove the finished recovery endpoint separately.** Identify actual 12.7 mm webbing/cord, eye and seam construction, thread, connector, chafe sleeve, contact width and saddle radius (currently only ~6 mm concept). Test the assembled eye over the actual saddle and attachment under the recalculated complete-vehicle recovery demand and asymmetric/dynamic cases, with temperature and moisture conditioning. Record seam, bend, connector and body-transfer failure mode; seller raw-cord or loop claims alone do not close it.
5. **Resolve current motor service geometry.** Obtain a current 78010 manufacturer/lot drawing or measure owner-supplied unmodified hardware for case, thrust shoulder, nozzle, ejection end and retainer envelope. Check hot clearances and removal with the actual retainer and thermal barrier without using the motor case as structure. Manufacturer application/retention instructions and later owner inspection must agree before an accepted package.
