## What a pass or failure would establish

S1's ten room strips and six process joints answer whether the specific lot and simple wood/paper process provide repeatable useful evidence at the required metrology quality. Five specimens/cell are screening, not a population allowable. Directional effective flexural response is not automatically transverseG or tensile strength. Failure, damaged substrate, nonlinearity or an uncertainty-dominated result stops root spending. See the [finalized quotation/acceptance protocol](protocol.md) for actual load caps, held-out data, finite supports, contact force, replication and stage stops.

S2 is conditional on a defined construction that can meet retained screens; the unchanged failed AD1 is not admitted by this package. The root method must preserve331.125 N/8.83 N·m and actual tab/tube/ring/body compliance. A two-point input alone does not establish distributed-panel tip motion. A **complete corresponding root measurement replaces modeled root terms**, never duplicates them. Total movement plus justified uncertainty must remain≤1 mm; strength screens remain unchanged. Thermal system, recovery opening/finished harness, motor/retainer/liner, installed flutter and flight-rate behavior are separate gates even if a static article passes.
