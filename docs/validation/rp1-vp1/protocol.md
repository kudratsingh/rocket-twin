# VP1 executable-method specification for quotation and later authorization

**Status: inquiry-ready; no execution authorized.** A laboratory must accept the specific fixture, substrate/process and uncertainty requirements in writing before any paid work. Its capability page is not method validation. This is one staged ordinary-plywood/WEST process programme, not automatic testing of AD1, FP1 and FR1 or permission for a new fin variant. [D1](../../wo03-rp1-load-attachment-decision.md) remains governing. Negative results stop advancement; missing data is not a pass.

## Stage S0 — bounded documentation and method/price agreement

Send only the four product drafts and one laboratory RFQ after owner authorization. Ask for existing stock/manufacturer/typical information and practical process guidance, not guaranteed aerospace allowables or custom-rocket certification. One follow-up on business day5; close documentation on day10. A supplier saying it has no E/G/hot/peel data is a useful completed answer. Move those gaps into this finite test scope. Do not request an open-ended certification programme or paid consultation.

The selected test material is Aircraft Spruce02-20850 nominal6 mm twelve-ply Finnish birch, with WEST105-A/206-A neat wet-out and406-2-filled non-sag fillets. It directly investigates a conventional, integral through-wall fin and avoids carbon handling, thin secondary films and skin-termination failure mechanisms. It is a test choice only. Existing AD1's estimated5 MPa/1 mm screen fails at the required load; neither stock purchase nor a successful coupon automatically admits that geometry. Any later material-screen revision needs explicit evidence and a recorded decision. Carbon alternatives remain separate.

## S1 — inexpensive information before an installed article

**People:** the owner may inspect/package stock after separate physical-work approval. Recommended cutting, cure/process witnesses and calibrated loading are by a contracted mechanical-testing laboratory. The RFQ includes these tasks; no workshop, cutting tools, scales, indicators, guards or fixtures are assumed owner stock. The home alternative below is a lower-accuracy triage only and is not the recommended equipment purchase.

**Stock:** one sheet/lot; photograph face grain, manufacturer/lot markings and defects. Map thickness at nine points per strip, width at five points; document mass/dimensions/temperature/RH. Proposed incoming5.8–6.2 mm and density750 kg/m³ remain unverified inspection/model windows, not vendor tolerances. If stock falls outside them, stop the source-matching model interpretation; do not plane it or relabel it compliant. Thickness/density records can support an explicit later spec update, never an automatic credit.

**Bare wood:** ten270×25 mm strips at actual thickness: five lengthwise along face grain, five across. Select locations across the sheet before looking for the stiffest pieces. Three per direction are calibration specimens and two per direction held-out evaluation specimens, labeled before loading. Condition at23±2°C and40–60% RH for at least48 h and until two consecutive24 h mass changes are≤0.2%. If not stable in seven days, stop and report; RH is not a direct wood-moisture measurement.

**Why the longer strip:** D1's150×25/60–120 mm spans were provisional. A240 mm primary span amplifies bending signal, reducing the cost of useful stiffness information. A180 mm span is held out;120 mm is a diagnostic only. This changes the specimen method, not the vehicle geometry,1 mm limit, material screen or load. Do not infer transverse/rolling shear G from a noisy short-span subtraction. The programme measures effective directional flexural response; G remains unavailable unless the lab demonstrates identifiability with a suitable additional method and separate quote.

**Fixture:** two parallel, freely seating cylindrical anvils nominal6 mm diameter, measured span240±0.1 mm, central aligned6 mm loading nose; loading line across the25 mm width. Do not pinch or glue the strip to its supports. Reference midspan displacement directly to the support line through an independent deflectometer/frame, not actuator/crosshead travel. Document contact radius, alignment, nose/support indentation, friction, indicator contact force and any support lift/slip. Re-seat and reverse a control; disagreement beyond the error budget invalidates the fixture. Inspect contact damage before treating a curve as material stiffness. Manufacturer references: [Instron direct deflection](https://www.instron.com/en/products/testing-accessories/flexure-fixtures/three-point-bend-test-fixtures/), [Shimadzu on compliance/indentation](https://www.shimadzu.com/an/apl/9683/index.html). Their plastics examples guide metrology; this custom plywood screen does not claim ASTM/ISO conformity.

Use at least five load levels and three load/unload cycles within the linear range. Start low; select levels from actual dimensions so estimated outer-fibre stress stays≤4 MPa and displacement/span≤1/50 during the stiffness screen. For a rectangular three-point strip `sigma=3PL/(2bt²)`, so `P<=2bt²×4MPa/(3L)`; do not simply apply10 N to every minimum-thickness bar. At25×5.8 mm,240 mm span this cap is9.344 N **before measurement uncertainty**. The already applied factor3 on the rocket load is not applied again to these measured loads or material stress. A force overload, visible damage, hysteresis or nonlinearity invalidates a linear fit; stop rather than extrapolate it.

After every held-out elastic evaluation is complete, the **same ten strips** enter the quoted sacrificial capacity phase, so no extra sample order is hidden. Load each at the agreed240 mm span to failure,100 N force or displacement/span1/20, whichever occurs first; stop and label a force/displacement-limited result as censored rather than inventing an ultimate strength. Record the load/displacement curve, nominal outer-fibre stress, contact effects and failure mode; if large deflection/indentation invalidates the simple stress formula, do not report it as intrinsic material strength. This phase is laboratory-only. At minimum5.8 mm, reaching the retained5 MPa nominal screen would require11.681 N at240 mm before uncertainty, above the stiffness-screen load cap; these are separate phases. All five specimens per direction provide strength/survival data after the calibration/evaluation sequence, not a population lower allowable. The two held-out specimens remain untouched by destructive loads until their elastic evaluation is finished. A flexural witness strength is not tensile, compression or shear strength. Failure below a required screen, invalid geometry or a censored result below the relevant demand stops construction admission. Record all negative results; no material screen is raised automatically.

**Process witnesses:** six T-joints, three wood-to-motor-tube and three wood-to-body. Each uses an actual60 mm tube segment and a25 mm axial-length plywood tab/witness in the recorded curved substrate, with nominal6 mm fillet legs (minimum5.8 if source-matching) and measured cured geometry. These are joint/process screens, not replacement areas to paste into the installed model. Record actual contact/bond area and the lever arm of the applied force. Apply a separately calibrated lateral load through the tab and inspect paper tear, wood split, peel, fillet cracking and residual movement. No invented1/3 sharing or uniform peel capacity. The lab must define this witness jig and measure substrate bending/contact before execution; a flat lap on a different paper is not equivalent. After the low-load/process inspection, all six witnesses are sacrificially loaded to failure or500 N maximum, whichever occurs first, with a guarded fixture and recorded lever/mode. Quote these curves explicitly; a machine-limit result is censored. Mean bond stress must use the measured actual contact area and does not establish a peel allowable or uniform load sharing. Do not substitute a higher force limit without a separate decision.

Prepare under current manufacturer instructions/SDS. Match GroupA resin/hardener;5:1 volume or5.36:1 weight, not5:1 weight. Prime/check pumps; pre-wet porous surfaces without soaking away the wall. Confirm tube finish/abrasion with suppliers; do not remove paper by a generic aggressive sanding rule. Use406-2 to manufacturer-described non-sag consistency, then record actual filler/mixed-resin masses so the same process repeats. No default30–50µm structural film is imposed on ordinary wood/paper. Measure accessible gaps, fillet legs, actual coverage and masses; section one sacrificial witness for voids/wet-out if included in the quote. Proposed cure: seven days22±2°C before conditioning, subject to WEST confirmation. This is not a qualified cure-time/strength claim. An unsupported preparation/cure or unrepeatable process stops the next stage.

**Data-quality gate:** written uncertainty budget including calibration, force contact/tare, span, thickness cubed, support/nose motion, drift and repeatability. For laboratory curves, expanded displacement uncertaintyU95≤0.01 mm and inferred effective flexural-compliance uncertainty≤10% over the fitted interval. Predict held-out180 mm results with interval from the calibrated method; every held-out result must lie in that predeclared uncertainty interval and the interval must remain≤10%, not widened after seeing failure. No fitted convenient root spring. Report specimen scatter separately from instrument uncertainty; five samples are screening evidence, not a statistical lower allowable. If uncertainty/hysteresis invalidates the method, stop S1 and return one corrected-method decision; do not buy the full root programme.

## S2 — conditioned samples first; a separate hold before root articles

A source-matching ordinary6 mm fin with estimated5 MPa still fails its existing strength screen. Before root construction/loading, either one simple correction must pass retained analytical screens, or an evidence-supported material-limit revision must be explicitly adopted. S1 surviving a load is not that decision. Stop here if neither is supported. The stock reserve below is for a source-matching representative article, not automatic admission of unchanged failed AD1. Changed dimensions require an updated article drawing/mass ledger before execution.

S2a may proceed under a separately approved quote if S1 supports further evidence gathering, even while root admission remains open. Add20 strips and12 process joints, including the same elastic/held-out and subsequent sacrificial phases and caps as S1: the same5 specimens per direction for each of dry45°C and humid45°C. Condition dry samples at45±1°C with recorded humidity; humid samples at45±1°C/90±5% RH to the same two-day≤0.2% mass-change criterion, maxseven days before replan. Record substrate temperatures before and during loading and transfer loss/gradient. Such specimen tests may straddle45°C; they do not revise the vehicle's unchanged45°C screen or demonstrate its motor/heat-soak thermal case. Material hot/wet exposure and the thermal model are distinct gates.

**S2b: three independent root builds are a separate hold after S2a and the construction/material admission decision.** Do not buy/fabricate the root articles just because conditioned coupons were authorized. They follow the D1 240 mm local article. Actual stations980–1220, tube1055–1220, forward ring1060–1072.2, tab1080.2–1189.6, aft ring1190–1196;0.4 mm nominal aft adhesive gap is not a butt stop. Retain conventional three integral fins/tabs and all applicable braces/fillets of the specific admitted definition; no carbon substitutions, new rigid motor mandrel or front-tab ring contact. Count and weigh every piece of paper, wood, wet-out/fillet resin, filler, finish and protection; replicate the knot50×20×14 mm/route keep-out with a labeled dummy, not a claimed finished-harness fit. No live motor/retainer or recovery-opening load is needed for this fin test. Those stay separate.

The quoted fixture must react through the body, leaving the motor tube and rings free to deform as defined. Record actual grip/saddle geometry, pressure/slip/crush checks and nearby body/motor/ring movement. Two controlled grip settings/configurations characterize sensitivity: if the measured root compliance changes beyond combined uncertainty or10%, the boundary is decision-driving and the result cannot transfer to the vehicle without a supported compliance model. No fixture rigidity is called installed strength. Measure global/fixture motion independently; unmeasured whole-body bending remains unavailable rather than being subtracted as zero.

**Inputs:** retain331.125 N/8.83 N·m once-factored. A characterized two-point yoke at10/40 mm can apply147.166667/183.958333 N. Verify actual reactions with temporary individual channels, pad centroids and friction; their ratio cannot be assumed from an ideal lever. For independent compliance identification, a10 mm single-line pattern up to331.125 N gives3.31125 N·m, and a40 mm pattern up to220.75 N gives8.83 N·m. Both stay within declared force/moment maxima; do not apply331 N at40 mm and accidentally demand13.245 N·m. Use both signs, staged25/50/75/100% loads, documented10 s holds/60 s final hold, stop for cracking, peel, slip, residual motion or force loss. Quasi-static actuator speed≤0.2 mm/min unless the lab documents another justified protocol; slow loading does not qualify flight-rate behavior.

Measure body-root translation/rotation and motor/ring/body motions with independent calibrated channels. Require U95≤1% each on appliedforce/moment and≤0.01 mm tip-equivalent combined displacement. A40 mm angular metrology baseline is permitted only on a validated non-stiffening rigid target or equivalent calibrated optical method; two points on a bending fin cannot simply be treated as a rigid rotation. Rotation over that baseline amplifies two displacement errors; show that propagation rather than assuming each0.003 mm indicator makes the combined result accurate. Repeat three independent builds; perform room then dry45 then humid45 on the same articles only as a **cumulative-exposure screening sequence**, not independent virgin hot-strength allowables. If any stage damages an article, do not reuse it to claim later success.

A two-point yoke establishes the root-input pair only. It does **not** recreate distributed panel pressure or prove total tip movement. Before a complete-fin≤1 mm conclusion, the lab and primary must validate distributed-panel loading (or an independently checked fixture-to-model transformation) and the panel/material model. The quote must itemize that work or explicitly exclude complete-fin acceptance. Combine a validated distributed-panel prediction with exactly the measured corresponding root term, plus any remaining global-body compliance and uncertainty. Never add the entire measured root on top of FP1's0.505505 mm tab and0.100 mm allowance. No root response or unmeasured support is assigned a convenient stiffness.

**Acceptance:** `total tip displacement + U95 <=1 mm` under the retained equivalent load, retained strength/adhesive screens, no damage/slip/peel and valid conditional properties/geometry. A near-threshold result whose uncertainty crosses1 mm is inconclusive/failed admission, not rounded up to a pass. Local witness or article survival alone is not a complete vehicle pass. Thermal matrix, recovery/finished cord, motor seat/liner, installed flutter, physical inspection, WO-10 and flight release stay open.

## Home-workshop alternative — not the recommended purchase

A270×25 mm,240 mm-span bare strip under≤9.344 N atminimum5.8 mm can provide effective-flexure triage, with force reduced for uncertainty. An indicator bridge references the support line; a safe calibrated mass/hanger is caught within10 mm, never34 kg hung for the full-root test. Record hanger mass, local-gravity uncertainty, indicator contact-force variation, support movement and calibration; a nominal1 kg weight is not a certified load. The economical2046A reads0.01 mm but has±0.013 mm accuracy and up to1.4 N contact force. The longer span improves signal, yet does not identify coreG or peel. Limit triage uncertainty to35%; if the signal cannot support that bound, stop and use a quoted service rather than adjusting a model spring. This relaxed **triage precision** does not change any vehicle acceptance threshold. No home result establishes a hot material allowable or root acceptance.

The cost register shows the priced tool basket and missing cutting/guard/reference-frame/hanger costs without any owner-stock credit. It does not call a catalogue stand a safe finished fixture. Home fabrication remains worthwhile for the eventual custom rocket, while metrology that cannot meet its error budget belongs with supervised equipment.
